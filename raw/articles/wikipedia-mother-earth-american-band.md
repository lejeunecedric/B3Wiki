**Mother Earth** was an eclectic American [blues rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blues_rock "Blues rock") band formed in 1967 in [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California "California"), fronted by singer [Tracy Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_Nelson_(singer) "Tracy Nelson (singer)").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(American_band)#cite_note-Larkin60-1)

Nelson, who hailed from [Madison, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison,_Wisconsin "Madison, Wisconsin"), began her career as a solo artist, but formed the Mother Earth ensemble after moving to [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco "San Francisco").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(American_band)#cite_note-Larkin60-1) The group performed at the [Avalon Ballroom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avalon_Ballroom "Avalon Ballroom") and [Fillmore Auditorium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fillmore "The Fillmore") in the late 1960s, and was included on the [soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soundtrack "Soundtrack") to the 1968 film _[Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution_(1968_film) "Revolution (1968 film)")_.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(American_band)#cite_note-2) The group signed to [Mercury Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_Records "Mercury Records"), recording four albums.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(American_band)#cite_note-Larkin60-1) [Mike Bloomfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Bloomfield "Mike Bloomfield") played guitar on their 1968 release _Living with the Animals_,[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(American_band)#cite_note-Larkin60-1) and [Boz Scaggs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boz_Scaggs "Boz Scaggs") contributed to their 1969 release _Make A Joyful Noise_. In addition to blues, the early incarnation of the group displayed influences from gospel, R&B, jazz, country and even a touch of psychedelia. After the first album, Mother Earth moved their base of operations from the Bay Area to a farm outside of [Nashville, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville,_Tennessee "Nashville, Tennessee").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(American_band)#cite_note-Larkin60-1) The nucleus of the band solidified around Nelson, keyboardist Andy McMahon and guitarist John "Toad" Andrews.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(American_band)#cite_note-3) Their manager and producer was Travis Rivers. Nelson was an astute judge of up-and-coming songwriters and was an early supporter of then largely unknown names like [John Hiatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hiatt "John Hiatt"), [Steve Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Young_(musician) "Steve Young (musician)"), and [Eric Kaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Kaz "Eric Kaz"). Mother Earth's version of Young's " [Seven Bridges Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Bridges_Road "Seven Bridges Road")" predates the [Eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eagles_(band) "Eagles (band)")' cover by about nine years. After two LPs with [Reprise Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reprise_Records "Reprise Records") and one with [Columbia Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_Records "Columbia Records") the ensemble continued to tour as Nelson's backup band but did not record anymore.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(American_band)#cite_note-Larkin60-1) They finally called it quits in early 1977. Tracy Nelson meanwhile returned to recording as a solo artist in 1974, issuing LPs on [Atlantic Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Records "Atlantic Records"), [MCA Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MCA_Records "MCA Records"), [Adelphi Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adelphi_Records "Adelphi Records"), [Flying Fish Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flying_Fish_Records "Flying Fish Records"), and others.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(American_band)#cite_note-Larkin60-1)

## Members

1967: [Tracy Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_Nelson_(singer) "Tracy Nelson (singer)"), [Powell St. John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powell_St._John "Powell St. John"), George Rains, Wayne Talbert, Ira Kamin, Jance Garfat.

1968: Tracy Nelson, Powell St. John, John Andrews, Bob Arthur, George Rains, Luis Gasca, [Mark Naftalin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Naftalin "Mark Naftalin").
