## Last.fm band wiki
Active since 1993, the quartet from Piacenza (Piacenza, Emilia-Romagna, Italy) has established itself internationally as a reference point for the Hammond sound thanks to an explosive blend of funk, rock and soul interpreted in a unique, modern and original way. The collaboration with prestigious labels such as Acid Jazz (UK), Record Kicks (ITA), Hammondbeat (USA), Soundflat (GER), P-Vine (JPN), Area Pirata (ITA) and many others has allowed the Link Quartet to work with numerous Italian and foreign artists, continually reinventing its own sound, with an eye to the future and one anchored in the long tradition of the Hammond organ. Members listed: Paolo "Apollo" Negri (2001–present).

## Hammondbeat (Wikipedia)
Hammondbeat's origins as the JTQgroove fan site led to a recording project with The Link Quartet (Piacenza, Italy). The Link Quartet were greatly influenced by the sound established by the James Taylor Quartet 15 years earlier. Beat.It was the project (HBR-001, 2002 — the label's first title), and while JTQ influences can be heard, The Link Quartet found themselves leaders with 10 years or more professional experience from each founding member, and Paolo Negri on keys.

## IAJO / apollonegri.com (member history)
An instrumental 1960s jazz-oriented band; Paolo "Apollo" Negri joined in 1997 (after his prog trio Nice Price split). With the group he recorded two 45s for the Spanish label Animal Records and two albums (Episode 1 and Beat.it) and joined Hammondbeat Records. The band performed as support for the James Taylor Quartet, Brian Auger's Oblivion Express, Manu Chao, The Chocolate Watchband and many others, playing all around Italy, Europe and USA. Per apollonegri.com, early lineup changes before the first album: guitarist Cardini and drummer Bergonzi's spots were filled by Fausto Mazza and Vittorio Solinas, and the horn section was dropped, bringing the band down to 5.
