## Discogs profile
"Men from S.P.E.C.T.R.E. distillate mod sound to the clubs since 1997. The mix of soundtracks of rocker attacks, the club scene, and end time visions build up the sound of this band. Blow out your speakers with Hammond organ and fuzzed + phased guitars, while drums, bass and percussion mash up the background." Real names: Rolf Keller, Gerry Germann, Mario Janser, Robert Ebler, Stefan Saurer. "The Living Eye" is the third record, released November 2006 on the American label Hammondbeat Records. Previously the albums "With the finger on the trigger" (1998) and "Sugartown" (2002) were published on the Swiss cult label Sheep Records. Some tracks were released on compilations on Acid Jazz Records (UK), Hammondbeat (USA), Vitaminic (Italy), BipBip (Spain).

## AllMusic (The Living Eye review)
"This German instrumental combo is all about the organ (note the title of their record label), and The Living Eye contains 11 tracks of funky Hammond organ rock with some soul-jazz and lite-psych tendencies." (Note: AllMusic says German; Discogs/Swiss press place the band in Winterthur, Switzerland — see contradiction note.)

## Personnel (Sheep Records liner notes, "With the Finger on the Trigger", 1999)
Guitar: Gerry Germann; Organ: Mario Janser; Drums: Stefan Saurer; Bass: Rolf Keller; Sitar: Pandit Shankarmann; Percussion: Marius Brun del Re.
