Musical artist

**Anthony John Medeski** (born June 28, 1965) is an American [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz "Jazz") keyboard player and composer. Medeski is a veteran of New York's 1990s avant-garde jazz scene and is known popularly as a member of [Medeski Martin & Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medeski_Martin_&_Wood "Medeski Martin & Wood").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Medeski#cite_note-1) He plays the acoustic piano and an eclectic array of keyboards, including the [Hammond B3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammond_B3 "Hammond B3") organ, [melodica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melodica "Melodica"), [mellotron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mellotron "Mellotron"), [clavinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clavinet "Clavinet"), [ARP String Ensemble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARP_String_Ensemble "ARP String Ensemble"), [Wurlitzer electric piano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wurlitzer_electric_piano "Wurlitzer electric piano"), [Moog Voyager Synthesizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimoog_Voyager "Minimoog Voyager"), Wurlitzer 7300 Combo Organ, [Vox Continental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vox_Continental "Vox Continental") Baroque organ, and Yamaha CS-1 Synthesizer, among others. When playing acoustic piano, Medeski usually plays the [Steinway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steinway_&_Sons "Steinway & Sons") piano and is listed as a [Steinway Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steinway_Artist "Steinway Artist").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Medeski#cite_note-2)

## Biography

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=John_Medeski&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Biography")\]

Medeski was born in [Louisville, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisville,_Kentucky "Louisville, Kentucky"), and grew up in Florida. After studying piano as a child (starting when he was five years old), he began as a teenager to perform with musicians such as [Mark Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Murphy_(singer) "Mark Murphy (singer)") and [Jaco Pastorius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaco_Pastorius "Jaco Pastorius"). He attended [Pine Crest School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_Crest_School "Pine Crest School"). In 1983, after graduating from high school, he began studying piano at the [New England Conservatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England_Conservatory "New England Conservatory") in Boston, where he performed as a [sideman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sideman "Sideman") with [Dewey Redman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dewey_Redman "Dewey Redman"), [Billy Higgins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Higgins "Billy Higgins"), [Bob Mintzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Mintzer "Bob Mintzer"), [Alan Dawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Dawson "Alan Dawson"). Medeski attributes his early interest in playing improvised music and jazz to listening to [Oscar Peterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Peterson "Oscar Peterson").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Medeski#cite_note-3)

Medeski performs in a range of musical styles, from accessible groove based funk and jazz (such as the MMW albums and _[A Go Go](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Go_Go_(John_Scofield_album) "A Go Go (John Scofield album)")_ with [John Scofield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Scofield "John Scofield")) to more experimental music (including many of [John Zorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Zorn "John Zorn")'s projects: [Duras: Duchamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duras:_Duchamp "Duras: Duchamp"), [Interzone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interzone_(band) "Interzone (band)"), Liber Novus, Nova Express, [Dreamachines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreamachines "Dreamachines"), [Templars: In Sacred Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Templars:_In_Sacred_Blood "Templars: In Sacred Blood"), [At the Gates of Paradise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/At_the_Gates_of_Paradise "At the Gates of Paradise") etc. and collaborations with [David Fiuczynski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fiuczynski "David Fiuczynski")).

Medeski is best known for his work with [Medeski Martin & Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medeski_Martin_&_Wood "Medeski Martin & Wood") including drummer and percussionist [Billy Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Martin_(percussionist) "Billy Martin (percussionist)") and bassist [Chris Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Wood_(jazz_musician) "Chris Wood (jazz musician)"). The trio formed in New York in 1991 playing gigs at [The Village Gate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Village_Gate "The Village Gate").[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Medeski#cite_note-4) Since the mid-1990s they have toured steadily in both national and international funk, jazz, and jam music scenes. The band has collaborated with such musicians as [Iggy Pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iggy_Pop "Iggy Pop"), [Trey Anastasio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trey_Anastasio "Trey Anastasio") and [John Scofield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Scofield "John Scofield").

Medeski produced and was featured on the [Dirty Dozen Brass Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirty_Dozen_Brass_Band "Dirty Dozen Brass Band")'s 1999 release, _[Buck Jump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buck_Jump "Buck Jump")_.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Medeski#cite_note-5) Also in 1999, he played the organ on two tracks of the [Morphine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morphine_(band) "Morphine (band)") album _[The Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Night_(Morphine_album) "The Night (Morphine album)")_.

In 2000, Medeski became a member of [The Word](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Word_(band) "The Word (band)"), a bluesy gospel style project with members [Robert Randolph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Randolph_(guitarist) "Robert Randolph (guitarist)") (pedal steel) from [Robert Randolph and the Family Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Randolph_and_the_Family_Band "Robert Randolph and the Family Band"), brothers [Luther Dickinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luther_Dickinson "Luther Dickinson") (guitar) and [Cody Dickinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cody_Dickinson "Cody Dickinson") (drums), and Chris Chew (bass) of [North Mississippi Allstars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Mississippi_Allstars "North Mississippi Allstars"). The band released a self-titled album in 2001 and toured in 2001 and 2002. The band reunited in 2005 for a performance at the [Bonnaroo Music Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonnaroo_Music_Festival "Bonnaroo Music Festival"), and they embarked on tour again in late 2007.

He has also occasionally performed with [Phil Lesh and Friends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Lesh_and_Friends "Phil Lesh and Friends").

Medeski was a guest on [Marian McPartland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marian_McPartland "Marian McPartland")'s _[Piano Jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piano_Jazz "Piano Jazz")_ radio program on September 27, 2005. During the show he talked at length about his formal training on the piano, his approach to playing music, and some of the musicians that he admires the most. He performed several songs by [Thelonious Monk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thelonious_Monk "Thelonious Monk") and other composers, as well as the [Medeski, Martin & Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medeski,_Martin_&_Wood "Medeski, Martin & Wood") classic "Bubblehouse". The show was released on compact disc on April 4, 2006.

Medeski was also an inaugural member of the Independent Music Awards' judging panel to support independent artists.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Medeski#cite_note-6)

On July 14, 2006, John Medeski & The Itch performed their debut gig at the [All Good Music Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Good_Music_Festival "All Good Music Festival") in [Masontown, West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masontown,_West_Virginia "Masontown, West Virginia"). The Itch consists of [Eric Krasno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Krasno "Eric Krasno") (guitar) of [Soulive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soulive "Soulive") and [Adam Deitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Deitch "Adam Deitch") (drums) with Medeski playing a B3 Organ. Also in 2006, Medeski performed with [The Million Dollar Bashers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Million_Dollar_Bashers?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Million Dollar Bashers (page does not exist)") for the recording of the soundtrack to the [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan "Bob Dylan") biopic, _[I'm Not There](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Not_There "I'm Not There")_.

In 2008, Medeski was featured on guitarist [Will Bernard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Bernard "Will Bernard")'s _[Blue Plate Special](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Plate_Special_(Will_Bernard_album) "Blue Plate Special (Will Bernard album)")_ album.

In 2012, Medeski was featured on progressive rock band [Coheed and Cambria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coheed_and_Cambria "Coheed and Cambria")'s albums " [The Afterman: Ascension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Afterman:_Ascension "The Afterman: Ascension")" and " [The Afterman: Descension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Afterman:_Descension "The Afterman: Descension")," performing piano, clavinet and synthesizer parts.

In 2016, Medeski formed the [supergroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supergroup_(music) "Supergroup (music)") Saudade along with [Chino Moreno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chino_Moreno "Chino Moreno") of [Deftones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deftones "Deftones"), [Team Sleep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_Sleep "Team Sleep"), [Palms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palms_(band) "Palms (band)"), and [Crosses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crosses_(band) "Crosses (band)"); guitarist [Dr. Know](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Know_(guitarist) "Dr. Know (guitarist)") of [Bad Brains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Brains "Bad Brains"); bassist Chuck Doom of Crosses and Team Sleep; and drummer Mackie Jayson of [Cro-Mags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cro-Mags "Cro-Mags") and Bad Brains.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Medeski#cite_note-saudade-7)

In 2018, Medeski formed John Medeski's Mad Skillet with two members of the [Dirty Dozen Brass Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirty_Dozen_Brass_Band "Dirty Dozen Brass Band"). They recorded an album in New Orleans and toured subsequently.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Medeski#cite_note-8)

In 2023, Medeski composed and performed the original score for the new television series [The Curse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Curse_(American_TV_series) "The Curse (American TV series)"), created and written by [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder "Nathan Fielder") and [Benny Safdie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benny_Safdie "Benny Safdie"), starring [Emma Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Stone "Emma Stone"), Fielder and Safdie.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Medeski#cite_note-9)
