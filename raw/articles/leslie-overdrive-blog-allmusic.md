## Band bio (somvalvulado, quoting band materials)
Leslie Overdrive is a Danish band formed in 2005: Kapper-Dapper on drums, Craig "Zilla" Kristensen on bass, "Dynamite" Mads Nielsen on guitar and "Reverend" Kristian Jørgensen on the Hammond organ. The quartet, despite coming from the cold countries, produces a hot sound full of funk and soul.

Band origin myth (quoted): "In the beginning God created the four men... And God said, 'Let there be Hammond,' and there was Hammond... He called the Hammond 'funk,' and the Leslie he called 'soul.'" Kapper and Craig met in 2003 through a mutual love of swirling Hammond; in early 2005 Kristian joined their former cabaret project "Funksovs", and a few months later, with Mads, the four turned in a new direction.

## Debut album
With The Hammond, In The Beauty (Hammondbeat HBL-007, 2007) — funk originals plus covers of The Beatles' "Drive My Car" and Jaco Pastorius' "The Chicken". Single: A Tad Askew b/w Oily (AllMusic).

## Name note
The band is named after the Leslie speaker/overdriven-Hammond sound — i.e., the whole sonic identity of the [[hammond-b3]] + [[leslie-speaker]] rig compressed into a band name.
