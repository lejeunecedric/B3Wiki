**Mother Earth** were an English [acid jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acid_jazz "Acid jazz") [outfit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_ensemble "Musical ensemble") based in London. The band members were Matt Deighton on [guitar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guitar "Guitar") and vocals, Bryn Barklam on [Hammond organ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammond_organ "Hammond organ"), Chris White on [drums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drums "Drums") and Neil Corcoran on [bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_guitar "Bass guitar"). Shauna Green was the lead singer on the first album. Prior to their debut live performance, where they played alongside another debutant band [Jamiroquai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamiroquai "Jamiroquai"), they started out as a studio project in 1991[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(British_band)#cite_note-BBC-1) with [Paul Weller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Weller "Paul Weller") (on "Almost Grown"), James Taylor of the [James Taylor Quartet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Taylor_Quartet "James Taylor Quartet") and Simon Bartholomew from the [Brand New Heavies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brand_New_Heavies "Brand New Heavies") as contributors.

They released three studio albums and one live album. After they disbanded in 1996,[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(British_band)#cite_note-BBC-1) two retrospective albums were released in 2001 and 2004.

## Subsequent activities

Deighton subsequently played guitar for [Paul Weller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Weller "Paul Weller")'s band, and played rhythm guitar in [Oasis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oasis_(band) "Oasis (band)") when [Noel Gallagher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noel_Gallagher "Noel Gallagher") left mid-tour in 2000.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(British_band)#cite_note-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(British_band)#cite_note-3)

Since 1996, Matt Deighton has released several critically acclaimed solo-albums.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(British_band)#cite_note-4)

Bryn Barklam went on to record with [The Chords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chords_(British_band) "The Chords (British band)") and play organ for the [Buzzcocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buzzcocks "Buzzcocks") at the Sex Pistols reunion gig at Finsbury Park.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Earth_(British_band)#cite_note-5) His current band is an instrumental trio consisting of organ, drums and guitar, and is named Captain Hammond.
