**Emerson, Lake & Palmer** (informally known as **ELP**) were  an English [progressive rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_rock "Progressive rock") [supergroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supergroup_(music) "Supergroup (music)") formed in London in 1970.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-3) The band consisted of [Keith Emerson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Emerson "Keith Emerson") (keyboards) of [the Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nice "The Nice"), [Greg Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Lake "Greg Lake") (vocals, bass, guitars, producer) of [King Crimson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Crimson "King Crimson"), and [Carl Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Palmer "Carl Palmer") (drums, percussion) of [Atomic Rooster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_Rooster "Atomic Rooster"). With nine [RIAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recording_Industry_Association_of_America "Recording Industry Association of America")-certified gold record albums in the US,[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-RIAA-4) and an estimated 48 million records sold worldwide,[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-5) they were one of the most popular and commercially successful progressive rock groups of the 1970s,[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-6)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-7) with a musical sound including adaptations of classical music with [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz "Jazz") and symphonic rock elements, dominated by Emerson's flamboyant use of the [Hammond organ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammond_organ "Hammond organ"), [Moog synthesizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moog_synthesizer "Moog synthesizer"), and piano (although Lake wrote several acoustic songs for the group).[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-BSS_Liner-8)

The band came to prominence following their performance at the [Isle of Wight Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Wight_Festival_1970 "Isle of Wight Festival 1970") in August 1970. In their first year, the group signed with [E.G. Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.G._Records "E.G. Records") (who distributed the band's records through [Island Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_Records "Island Records") in the United Kingdom, and [Atlantic Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Records "Atlantic Records") in North America), and released _[Emerson, Lake & Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer_(album) "Emerson, Lake & Palmer (album)")_ (1970) and _[Tarkus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarkus "Tarkus")_ (1971), both of which reached the UK top five. The band's success continued with _[Pictures at an Exhibition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pictures_at_an_Exhibition_(Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer_album) "Pictures at an Exhibition (Emerson, Lake & Palmer album)")_ (1971), _[Trilogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trilogy_(Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer_album) "Trilogy (Emerson, Lake & Palmer album)")_ (1972), and _[Brain Salad Surgery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brain_Salad_Surgery "Brain Salad Surgery")_ (1973, released on ELP's own [Manticore Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manticore_Records "Manticore Records") label). After a three-year break, Emerson, Lake & Palmer released _[Works Volume 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Works_Volume_1 "Works Volume 1")_ (1977) and _[Works Volume 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Works_Volume_2 "Works Volume 2")_ (1977). After _[Love Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Beach "Love Beach")_ (1978), the group disbanded in 1979.

The band re-formed partially in the 1980s as [Emerson, Lake & Powell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Powell "Emerson, Lake & Powell") featuring [Cozy Powell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cozy_Powell "Cozy Powell") in place of Palmer, who was, by then, a member of [Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_(band) "Asia (band)"). [Robert Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Berry "Robert Berry") then replaced Lake while Palmer returned, forming [3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3_(1980s_band) "3 (1980s band)"). In 1991, the original trio re-formed and released two more albums, _[Black Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Moon_(album) "Black Moon (album)")_ (1992) and _[In the Hot Seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Hot_Seat "In the Hot Seat")_ (1994), and toured at various times between 1992 and 1998. Their final performance took place in 2010 at the [High Voltage Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Voltage_Festival "High Voltage Festival") in London to commemorate the band's 40th anniversary. Both Emerson and Lake died in 2016,[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-keithemersondeath-9)[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-billboard_emerson_death-10)[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-BBC08122016-11) leaving Palmer as the only surviving member of the band.

Stylistically, the band was known for "\[combining\] heavy riffs with [classical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_music "Classical music") influences." Bruce Eder of [AllMusic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AllMusic "AllMusic") wrote, "Their flamboyance on record and in the studio echoed the best work of the heavy metal bands of the era, proving that classical rockers could compete for that arena-scale audience."[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-12)

## History

### 1969–1970: Formation and first gigs

By the end of 1969, [the Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nice "The Nice") keyboardist [Keith Emerson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Emerson "Keith Emerson") and [King Crimson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Crimson "King Crimson") bassist/vocalist [Greg Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Lake "Greg Lake") were looking to leave their respective groups and form a new band. The pair first met in New York City and discussed the possibility of forming one together; they met again in December 1969 when the Nice and King Crimson were billed together for concerts at the [Fillmore West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fillmore_West "Fillmore West") in San Francisco. During a [soundcheck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soundcheck "Soundcheck") before one of the shows, Emerson described the first time he and Lake played together: "Greg was moving a bass line and I played the piano in back and Zap! It was there."[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-classicrockarchive-13) When the Nice split in March 1970 and Lake left King Crimson a month later, the pair began the search for a drummer, which turned out to be a difficult process.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-NME70-14) They initially approached [Mitch Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_Mitchell "Mitch Mitchell"), who was at a loose end following the break-up of [the Jimi Hendrix Experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jimi_Hendrix_Experience "The Jimi Hendrix Experience") and suggested a jam session take place among the three of them and guitarist [Jimi Hendrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimi_Hendrix "Jimi Hendrix"). The session never happened, but it led to the press later reporting rumours of a planned [supergroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supergroup_(music) "Supergroup (music)") named HELP, an acronym for "Hendrix Emerson Lake Palmer", which Lake later debunked.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-15) As part of auditions for drummers at a studio by [Soho Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soho_Square "Soho Square"),[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-unknown1971-main-16) Emerson's manager, [Tony Stratton Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stratton_Smith "Tony Stratton Smith"), suggested [Carl Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Palmer "Carl Palmer") of [Atomic Rooster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_Rooster "Atomic Rooster") and previously [the Crazy World of Arthur Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Crazy_World_of_Arthur_Brown "The Crazy World of Arthur Brown").[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-goldmine1996-17) Palmer turned up for a session and enjoyed the chemistry, but was reluctant to commit as Atomic Rooster were starting to gain attention in Europe. Emerson and Lake persisted, and after several weeks, Palmer agreed to join.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerson,_Lake_&_Palmer#cite_note-goldmine1996-17)
