# Query: the modern (active) organ scene — who is carrying the B-3 today?

## The soul-jazz revival wave

The 2010s–2020s revival of the classic organ trio runs largely through a handful of boutique funk/soul labels — above all **Colemine Records** (Loveland, Ohio) and its subsidiaries **Karma Chief** and **Colemine's roster of organ bands** — a direct descendant of the Daptone-era analog-soul movement (Sugarman 3's Adam Scone is a scene linchpin). Colemine grew from a CD-and-vinyl operation into the scene's center of gravity: [Delvon Lamarr Organ Trio](/entities/bands/delvon-lamarr-organ-trio.md), [Parlor Greens](/entities/bands/parlor-greens.md), The Sextones, GA-20 all orbit it.[^bandcampdaily-parlor-greens-emeralds] Outside the US: Zip Records (Netherlands) for [The Pulitzers](/entities/bands/the-pulitzers.md), Soul Messin' Records (Melbourne) for [Cookin' on 3 Burners](/entities/bands/cookin-on-3-burners.md), and self-run imprints elsewhere — a scattered ecosystem compared to the old [Hammondbeat Records](/entities/labels/hammondbeat.md) / acid-jazz labels of the 2000s wave.

**Discovery changed the game.** The 1960s trios were built on club residencies, AM radio singles and Blue Note linernotes; this generation was built on YouTube and KEXP live sessions (DLO3's *Live at KEXP!* is itself a charting album), Bandcamp preorders, vinyl-collector culture and playlist/streaming reach.[^wikipedia-delvon-lamarr-organ-trio]

**How the new generation differs from the 60s organ trios:**
- **No bassist** as a purist statement (DLO3), or bass pedals dropped entirely in favor of locked pocket drumming.
- **Vintage production as aesthetic** — tape machines and Tascam 388s, retro vinyl packaging, but distributed via streaming.
- **Rotating lineups under one bandleader-brand**, rather than stable working combos: DLO3 cycled through five drummers and three guitarists in a decade while the "Delvon Lamarr Organ Trio" name stayed constant.[^wikipedia-delvon-lamarr-organ-trio]
- **Cross-pollination**: players hop between scene projects (Jimmy James: True Loves → DLO3 → [Parlor Greens](/entities/bands/parlor-greens.md); Scone: Sugarman 3 → Scone Cash Players → Parlor Greens).
- **Gospel church roots reasserted** at the virtuoso end ([Cory Henry](/entities/players/cory-henry.md)), versus the jazz-club roots of Jimmy Smith's generation.
