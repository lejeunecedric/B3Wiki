# Harmonic Percussion

The B-3/C-3's 1954 addition, advertised as **"Touch-Response Percussion Control"** — and the reason the B-3 became *the* jazz organ rather than one model among many.

## Behavior
- Adds a short **decaying envelope** to a note as it is struck: choose **2nd** or **3rd harmonic**, fast or slow decay, soft or normal volume (the four percussion tabs).
- It is **monophonic** and only on the **upper manual** — play legato and the percussion only re-fires on the first note, which shapes the classic single-line jazz phrasing.
- Important trap: engaging percussion **removes the 1′ drawbar** from the upper manual's normal voice (the two share circuitry).

## Why it matters
That "bong"-like attack lets the organ imitate plucked/bowed articulation — [[jimmy-smith|Jimmy Smith]] built his horn-like phrasing on it. Combined with a [[leslie-speaker|Leslie]] on tremolo, it is the definitive Hammond jazz voice.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hammond-organ.md]
