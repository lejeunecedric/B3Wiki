# Drawbars & Additive Synthesis

Drawbars are sliders that set the volume of each harmonic component of the tone — nine per manual on console Hammonds. Pull out = louder, push in = silent.

## The footage system
Labels come from pipe-organ **stops** (physical pipe length ↔ pitch):
- **16′** one octave below the fundamental, **8′** the fundamental, **4′ / 2′ / 1′** one, two, three octaves above
- **5⅓′** the quint (2nd harmonic), plus other mixture-style harmonics/subharmonics

Each individual drawbar sounds flute-pure; timbre comes from **mixing** them — the Hammond is a real-time **additive synthesizer** you play with your fingertips while performing.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hammond-organ.md]

## Culture
- From 1969 onward the footage numbers were engraved on drawbar ends.
- Settings became a language: **888000000** (16′ + 5⅓′ + 8′ full out) = the classic [[jimmy-smith|Jimmy Smith]] sound.
- Players change settings mid-phrase — something impossible on a pipe organ and central to the Hammond's expressivity.
