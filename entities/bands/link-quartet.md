# The Link Quartet

Instrumental Hammond-led band from **Piacenza, Emilia-Romagna, Italy**, active since 1993 — "a reference point for the Hammond sound thanks to an explosive blend of funk, rock and soul interpreted in a unique, modern and original way".^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md] The flagship act of [[hammondbeat|Hammondbeat Records]] and the band that turned the label from a fan site into a recording operation.

## Story
- Founded in Piacenza in 1993 as an instrumental 1960s-jazz-oriented band.^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md]
- **Paolo "Apollo" Negri** joined in 1997 ([[paolo-negri|see player page]]); two 45s on the Spanish label Animal Records followed, then the albums *Episode 1* and *Beat.it*.^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md]
- The link to [[james-taylor-quartet|JTQ]] is explicit: they were greatly influenced by the sound JTQ established 15 years earlier, toured as JTQ's support act, and the Hammondbeat collaboration began as a recording project with them — *Beat.it* (HBR-001, 2002) being the label's first title, with founding members each carrying 10+ years of professional experience.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hammondbeat.md]^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md]
- Opened for JTQ, [[brian-auger|Brian Auger's]] Oblivion Express, Manu Chao, The Chocolate Watchband; toured Italy, Europe and the USA.^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md]

## Labels and discography
Acid Jazz (UK), Record Kicks (ITA), Hammondbeat (USA), Soundflat (GER), P-Vine (JPN), Area Pirata (ITA), Spinout Nuggets (UK).^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md]

- *Episode 1* (Animal, Spain)
- *Beat.it* (Hammondbeat HBR-001, 2002)
- *4* (Hammondbeat HBR-013, 2011)
- *Long Live The Link* — live (P-Vine, Japan, rec. 2007)
- *Hotel Constellation* (Hammondbeat HBR-016, 2014, with Miss Modus)
- "Green Puma" (Spinout Nuggets, 2020s)

Lineup (2007 live, per P-Vine credits): Paolo "Apollo" Negri (Hammond organ), Marco "Mayo" Murtas (guitar), Giulio "Link" Cardini (guitar), Renzo Bassi (bass), Tony "Face" Bacciocchi (drums), Alberto "Pato" Maffi (drums).^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md]

They are the strongest single proof that the [[hammond-b3]]-organ idiom survived as a living band tradition in Europe, alongside France's [[eddy-louiss|Eddy Louiss]] in the preceding generation.
