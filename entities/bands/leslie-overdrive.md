# Leslie Overdrive (Denmark)

Danish four-piece organ-funk band formed in 2005 — named, in a one-line summary of the whole [Hammond B-3](/entities/instruments/hammond-b3.md)-plus-[Leslie](/entities/instruments/leslie-speaker.md) rig, after the sound itself: Hammond "funk", Leslie "soul". Lineup: Kapper-Dapper (drums), Craig "Zilla" Kristensen (bass), "Dynamite" Mads Nielsen (guitar), "**Reverend**" Kristian Jørgensen (Hammond organ).[1]

## Story and records
- Kapper and Craig met in 2003 over "a mutual love of swirling Hammond"; with Kristian (from their cabaret project Funksovs) and Mads the band took its final shape in 2005.[1]
- Debut ***...With the Hammond, In the Beauty*** (Hammondbeat HBL-007, 2007, EP/CD) — funk originals plus covers of The Beatles' "Drive My Car" and Jaco Pastorius' "The Chicken"; followed by the single "A Tad Askew" b/w "Oily" (AllMusic).[1]

They are the Scandinavian corner of the [Hammondbeat](/entities/labels/hammondbeat.md) roster, alongside Italy's [Link Quartet](/entities/bands/link-quartet.md) and Switzerland's [Men From S.P.E.C.T.R.E.](/entities/bands/men-from-spectre.md) — and their guitarist Mads Nielsen appears as a guest on [Paolo 'Apollo' Negri's](/entities/players/paolo-negri.md) *A Bigger Tomorrow*.[1] The band's entire identity is an homage to the classic organ-trio-plus-Leslie format that [Jimmy Smith](/entities/players/jimmy-smith.md) established.
