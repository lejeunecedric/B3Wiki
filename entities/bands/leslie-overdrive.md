# Leslie Overdrive (Denmark)

Danish four-piece organ-funk band formed in 2005 — named, in a one-line summary of the whole [[hammond-b3]]-plus-[[leslie-speaker|Leslie]] rig, after the sound itself: Hammond "funk", Leslie "soul". Lineup: Kapper-Dapper (drums), Craig "Zilla" Kristensen (bass), "Dynamite" Mads Nielsen (guitar), "**Reverend**" Kristian Jørgensen (Hammond organ).^[raw/articles/leslie-overdrive-blog-allmusic.md]

## Story and records
- Kapper and Craig met in 2003 over "a mutual love of swirling Hammond"; with Kristian (from their cabaret project Funksovs) and Mads the band took its final shape in 2005.^[raw/articles/leslie-overdrive-blog-allmusic.md]
- Debut ***...With the Hammond, In the Beauty*** (Hammondbeat HBL-007, 2007, EP/CD) — funk originals plus covers of The Beatles' "Drive My Car" and Jaco Pastorius' "The Chicken"; followed by the single "A Tad Askew" b/w "Oily" (AllMusic).^[raw/articles/leslie-overdrive-blog-allmusic.md]

They are the Scandinavian corner of the [[hammondbeat|Hammondbeat]] roster, alongside Italy's [[link-quartet|Link Quartet]] and Switzerland's [[men-from-spectre|Men From S.P.E.C.T.R.E.]] — and their guitarist Mads Nielsen appears as a guest on [[paolo-negri|Paolo 'Apollo' Negri's]] *A Bigger Tomorrow*.^[raw/articles/leslie-overdrive-blog-allmusic.md] The band's entire identity is an homage to the classic organ-trio-plus-Leslie format that [[jimmy-smith]] established.
