# The Men From S.P.E.C.T.R.E.

Instrumental Hammond-organ rock band from **Winterthur, Switzerland**, distilling "mod sound... since 1997": Hammond organ, fuzzed and phased guitars over drums, bass and percussion, in a spy-soundtrack/70s-action aesthetic. Members: Rolf Keller, Gerry Germann, **Mario Janser (Hammond organ, synths, Fender Rhodes)**, Robert Ebler, Stefan Saurer.[1]

## Records
- *With the Finger on the Trigger* (Sheep Records, 1998/99) — "14 Hammond-driven, hip-shaking spy themes"[1]
- *Sugartown* (Sheep Records, 2002)
- ***The Living Eye*** (Hammondbeat HBR-007, 2006) — third album, 11 tracks of "funky Hammond organ rock with some soul-jazz and lite-psych tendencies" (AllMusic)[1]

Tracks also appear on compilations for Acid Jazz Records (UK), Hammondbeat (USA), Vitaminic (Italy), BipBip (Spain).[1]

## Contradictions
- **Country:** AllMusic calls them a "German instrumental combo"; Discogs and the Swiss press place them in Winterthur, Switzerland (Sheep Records was a Swiss label). The Swiss attribution is better sourced; flagged for review.

## Context
The most soundtrack-obsessed act on the [Hammondbeat](/entities/labels/hammondbeat.md) roster: where [The Link Quartet](/entities/bands/link-quartet.md) modernized the JTQ funk formula and [Leslie Overdrive](/entities/bands/leslie-overdrive.md) kept it soulful, S.P.E.C.T.R.E. leaned into the same 60s spy-film territory [JTQ](/entities/bands/james-taylor-quartet.md) itself mined on *Mission Impossible* — evidence of how broadly the organ-revival scene ranged beyond [Jimmy Smith](/entities/players/jimmy-smith.md)'s jazz template.
