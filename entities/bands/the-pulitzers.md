# The Pulitzers

**Correction to the original query:** The Pulitzers are not French — they are a **Dutch** Hammond organ funk quartet (Rotterdam scene). Organist **Frank Montis** (also of Montis, Goudsmit & Directie), guitarist **Bas van der Wal**, percussionist **Phil Martin**, and drummer **Salle de Jonge** (known for his work with soul singer Sabrina Starke). They are "young veterans" of the Dutch scene, rooted in The Meters, [Jimmy McGriff](/entities/players/jimmy-mcgriff.md) and Booker T. & the M.G.'s.[1]

Their debut album *Honk Honk* was recorded in 2022 at Epic Rainbow Unicorn Studio and released as Zip 343 on **Zip Records** in 2023 (digital March 2024) — instrumental funk 'n' soul, with a notable cover of "Don't Get Around Much Anymore".[1] They remain active: booked for the Saint-Paul Soul Funk Festival (France) in July 2026 alongside Cookin' on 3 Burners.[1] (festival source; confidence: medium)
