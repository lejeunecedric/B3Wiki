# The Pulitzers

**Correction to the original query:** The Pulitzers are not French — they are a **Dutch** Hammond organ funk quartet (Rotterdam scene). Organist **Frank Montis** (also of Montis, Goudsmit & Directie), guitarist **Bas van der Wal**, percussionist **Phil Martin**, and drummer **Salle de Jonge** (known for his work with soul singer Sabrina Starke). They are "young veterans" of the Dutch scene, rooted in The Meters, [[jimmy-mcgriff]] and Booker T. & the M.G.'s.^[raw/articles/flophouse-the-pulitzers-honk-honk.md]

Their debut album *Honk Honk* was recorded in 2022 at Epic Rainbow Unicorn Studio and released as Zip 343 on **Zip Records** in 2023 (digital March 2024) — instrumental funk 'n' soul, with a notable cover of "Don't Get Around Much Anymore".^[raw/articles/flophouse-the-pulitzers-honk-honk.md] They remain active: booked for the Saint-Paul Soul Funk Festival (France) in July 2026 alongside Cookin' on 3 Burners.^[raw/articles/flophouse-the-pulitzers-honk-honk.md] (festival source; confidence: medium)

See [[modern-organ-scene]] — the band slots into the European wing of the revival.
