# Parlor Greens

The "Jimmy James trio" the user asked about: a fresh organ trio on Colemine Records formed in 2025 by guitarist [[jimmy-james]] (ex-[[delvon-lamarr-organ-trio]], True Loves) with **Adam Scone** on Hammond organ (Scone Cash Players, The Sugarman 3, a Daptone-scene veteran) and **Tim Carman** (GA-20) on drums. The project was hatched when Carman told Colemine founder Terry Cole he wanted an organ trio; Cole called James and Scone directly. Their first studio jam, "West Memphis," was released as the debut single (July 2025), and the debut album *Emeralds* followed on Colemine/Karma Chief.^[raw/articles/bandcampdaily-parlor-greens-emeralds.md]

Note the inverted format: unlike DLO3, here the guitarist is the founding star with organ as the foil — a boogaloo-leaning take on the [[modern-organ-scene]] trio template.
