# Jimmy Smith (1925–2005)

American jazz organist whose B-3 playing, starting in the mid-1950s, defined the modern approach to the instrument and inspired a generation of organists.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hammond-organ.md]

## Why he matters here
- Popularized the **organ trio** format (organ + drums + guitar/sax) — club-friendly, big-band power at trio prices.
- His signature drawbar setting **888000000** (16', 5⅓', 8' fully out) is known as "the classic Jimmy Smith sound".
- Used the [[hammond-b3|B-3]]'s [[harmonic-percussion|harmonic percussion]] aggressively, treating the organ like a horn player treats lines — bass in the pedals/left hand, melody on top.
