# Stax Records

**Stax Records** is the Memphis soul label founded by **Jim Stewart** in **1957** as Satellite Records, renamed **Stax** in September 1961; with its sister label Volt it defined the Southern soul sound out of a converted movie theater on McLemore Avenue.[^wikipedia-stax-records]

## Why it matters to the Hammond story

Stax's house band, **Booker T. & the M.G.'s**, was one of the first racially integrated groups of session musicians in the South, and its keyboard chair — [Booker T. Jones](/entities/players/booker-t-jones.md) on the Hammond B-3 and piano — turned the church-organ voice into the engine of hit instrumental R&B. "Green Onions" (1962) is the single most famous Hammond-led hit of the 1960s and a textbook of restraint: drawbar-set groove, Leslie speed swells, and space.[^wikipedia-stax-records][^wikipedia-booker-t-jones]

Jones also played on hundreds of Stax sessions, making the B-3 a core texture of Otis Redding, Wilson Pickett, Sam & Dave and William Bell records — the bridge between gospel organ and rock-radio audiences.[^wikipedia-stax-records]
