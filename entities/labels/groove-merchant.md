# Groove Merchant

**Groove Merchant** was an American jazz and R&B label of the 1970s, **run by producer Sonny Lester** and distributed by Pickwick Records (later TK Records for a period); Lester later restructured it as the Lester Radio Corporation (LRC) and retains the back catalogue.[^wikipedia-groove-merchant] The label issued 34 albums in its "500 Series" (1971–74) plus 2200/3300/4000 series through 1976 — most of them lean, punchy soul-jazz sessions cut for a budget-market audience.[^wikipedia-groove-merchant]

## Why it matters to the Hammond story

Groove Merchant is a *second act* label for the soul-jazz organ greats who aged out of the Blue Note system: its roster lists **Jimmy McGriff**, **Lonnie Smith**, Groove Holmes, Reuben Wilson, O'Donel Levy and "Giants of the Organ" live compilations alongside Chick Corea, Buddy Rich and Lionel Hampton.[^wikipedia-groove-merchant]

- [Jimmy McGriff](/entities/players/jimmy-mcgriff.md) cut a run of fatback organ LPs here (*Fly Dude*, *Let's Stay Together*, compiled on *Flyin' Time*)[^wikipedia-groove-merchant][^wikipedia-jimmy-mcgriff]
- [Dr. Lonnie Smith](/entities/players/dr-lonnie-smith.md) recorded for Groove Merchant in the early 1970s between his Blue Note and CTI-era work[^wikipedia-dr-lonnie-smith]
