# Labels Index

> Auto-maintained list of pages in this folder. Main catalog: [[../index|index.md]].

- [[hammondbeat|Hammondbeat Records]] — Organ-sound record label (2002), grown from the JTQgroove acid-jazz fan site.
- [[blue-note-records|Blue Note Records]] — Jazz label founded 1939; codified the soul-jazz organ with Jimmy Smith and Dr. Lonnie Smith.
- [[stax-records|Stax Records]] — Memphis soul label (1957); Booker T. Jones's B-3 turned church organ into hit R&B.
- [[verve-records|Verve Records]] — Norman Granz's jazz label (1956); carried Jimmy Smith to mass popularity after Blue Note.
- [[colemine-records|Colemine Records]] — Ohio soul/funk indie (2007, incl. Karma Chief); flagship label of the modern organ-trio revival.
- [[groove-merchant|Groove Merchant]] — Sonny Lester's 1970s soul-jazz label; second act for McGriff and Lonnie Smith.
- [[zip-records|Zip Records]] — San Francisco/Amsterdam indie (1997); home of The Pulitzers' Hammond funk.
