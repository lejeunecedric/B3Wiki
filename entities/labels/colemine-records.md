# Colemine Records

**Colemine Records** is an American independent soul/funk label founded in **2007** in **Oxford, Ohio** (now based in **Loveland, Ohio**, alongside the owners' record store Plaid Room Records). Founder **Kelly Cole** — then a high-school biology teacher — literally drew the logo on a Subway napkin to release a record by his own band; his brother **Bob Cole** joined full-time at the end of 2014.^[raw/articles/colemine-records-about-us.md] In 2018 Colemine launched its rock-leaning imprint **Karma Chief Records**, whose first signing was the Grammy-nominated Black Pumas.^[raw/articles/colemine-records-about-us.md]

## Why it matters to the Hammond story

Colemine is the flagship label of the **modern organ-trio revival**, run "primarily as a curator" for artists too idiosyncratic for bigger indies.^[raw/articles/grammy-colemine-15-years.md]

- [[delvon-lamarr-organ-trio|Delvon Lamarr Organ Trio]] — Seattle soul-jazz trio; *I Told You So* (January 29, 2021) topped the Billboard contemporary-jazz chart and made the label's organ trio a streaming-era phenomenon^[raw/articles/wikipedia-delvon-lamarr-organ-trio.md]
- [[parlor-greens|Parlor Greens]] — Jimmy James's organ trio with Adam Scone and Tim Carman (*Emeralds*, 2025)^[raw/articles/bandcampdaily-parlor-greens-emeralds.md]
- Organ-adjacent roster: Orgone, Durand Jones & The Indications, Monophonics, GA-20^[raw/articles/grammy-colemine-15-years.md]
