# Colemine Records

**Colemine Records** is an American independent soul/funk label founded in **2007** in **Oxford, Ohio** (now based in **Loveland, Ohio**, alongside the owners' record store Plaid Room Records). Founder **Kelly Cole** — then a high-school biology teacher — literally drew the logo on a Subway napkin to release a record by his own band; his brother **Bob Cole** joined full-time at the end of 2014.[^colemine-records-about-us] In 2018 Colemine launched its rock-leaning imprint **Karma Chief Records**, whose first signing was the Grammy-nominated Black Pumas.[^colemine-records-about-us]

## Why it matters to the Hammond story

Colemine is the flagship label of the **modern organ-trio revival**, run "primarily as a curator" for artists too idiosyncratic for bigger indies.[^grammy-colemine-15-years]

- [Delvon Lamarr Organ Trio](/entities/bands/delvon-lamarr-organ-trio.md) — Seattle soul-jazz trio; *I Told You So* (January 29, 2021) topped the Billboard contemporary-jazz chart and made the label's organ trio a streaming-era phenomenon[^wikipedia-delvon-lamarr-organ-trio]
- [Parlor Greens](/entities/bands/parlor-greens.md) — Jimmy James's organ trio with Adam Scone and Tim Carman (*Emeralds*, 2025)[^bandcampdaily-parlor-greens-emeralds]
- Organ-adjacent roster: Orgone, Durand Jones & The Indications, Monophonics, GA-20[^grammy-colemine-15-years]
