# Hammondbeat Records (founded 2002)

American record label **dedicated to bands and fans of the organ sound** — not a genre label in the traditional sense, but one embracing every genre the organ has influenced. Also functions as an online community connecting fans to hundreds of organ bands and the indie labels that support them (discographies, downloads, web radio, forum).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hammondbeat.md]

## Origin
- Began in 1997 as **JTQgroove**, a fan website for the Hammond-led acid-jazz band the James Taylor Quartet (JTQ).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hammondbeat.md]
- The fan collective outgrew one artist and attracted next-generation organ-centric bands, leading to a recording project with The Link Quartet (Piacenza, Italy) — JTQ-influenced veterans with Paolo 'Apollo' Negri on keys.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hammondbeat.md]
- Business model: controls most of its catalog for both entertainment and marketing/synchronization licensing.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hammondbeat.md]

## Catalog highlights
- 2002 HBR-001: _Beat.it_ — The Link Quartet (the label's first title)
- Recurring artists: The Link Quartet, Paolo 'Apollo' Negri, The Special Agents, Phat Fred, The Men From S.P.E.C.T.R.E., Leslie Overdrive, Low Fidelity Jet-Set Orchestra, Captain Hammond, The Dansettes
- The acid-jazz band most responsible for the modern revival is [[james-taylor-quartet|the James Taylor Quartet]] (JTQ), founded 1985 around Hammond player James Taylor — the label itself began as their fan site.

- Labels like this are the institutional tail of the [[jimmy-smith]]-rooted organ-trio tradition — the acid-jazz/organ-revival scene that treats the [[hammond-b3|B-3]] sound as a genre-independent idiom (see [[dr-lonnie-smith]] for the jazz side of the same revival).
