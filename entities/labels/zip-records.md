# Zip Records

**Zip Records** is an independent label started in **1997** by president **Arthur Herman**, operating from **San Francisco, California** and **Amsterdam, Netherlands** (relocated/expanded to Amsterdam in 2013). From inception to today it has released roughly **125 titles** — about half licensed, half owned masters — distributed in the US/Canada via Sony/RED and in the Benelux via PIAS/Rough Trade.[1]

## Why it matters to the Hammond story

Zip is the Dutch-to-American conduit for the **new Hammond funk wave**: Dutch Hammond funk quartet [The Pulitzers](/entities/bands/the-pulitzers.md) (fronted by Frank Montis) released their album *Honk Honk* on Zip Records in 2023, plugging the Rotterdam band into the same international circuit as the acid-jazz revival documented on [Hammondbeat Records](/entities/labels/hammondbeat.md) and [Colemine](/entities/labels/colemine-records.md).[2] The label's wider roster leans retro-soul and guitar-pop, making it a niche but steady home for organ-led grooves outside the big indie system.
