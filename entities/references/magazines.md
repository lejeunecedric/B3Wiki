# Hammond & Organ Magazines

Print and online magazines that document the Hammond organ, the B-3 and jazz organ
playing: artist interviews, gear features and record reviews. Primary secondary
sources for ingestion into `raw/articles/`.

## Magazines

- **DownBeat** — US jazz monthly (since 1934); decades of B-3 coverage from Jimmy Smith
  onward, historic organ-trio reviews. https://downbeat.com
- **JazzTimes** — US jazz magazine (1970–2021 print; webzine after) with extensive
  organ-trio and soul-jazz features. https://jazztimes.com
- **Keyboard** — US keyboard magazine (1975–; print ended 2016, archive online); drawbar
  technique, Leslie pairing and clonewheel reviews. https://www.keyboardmag.com
- **Living Blues** — US magazine (since 1970) covering blues organists and church-to-club
  organ traditions. https://livingblues.com
- **Record Collector** — UK monthly with B-3 and soul-jazz record-collecting guides.
  https://recordcollectormag.com
