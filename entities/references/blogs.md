# Hammond & Organ Blogs and Communities

Independent blogs, webzines and community sites covering the Hammond organ and jazz
organ — from playing technique to collecting. Frequent ingestion targets for
`raw/articles/` (Reddit blocks scraping; prefer these).

## Blogs and communities

- **organissimo.org** — long-running jazz-organ forum and community (since 2001); primary
  source for player anecdotes and discography debates. Save threads under `raw/forums/`.
  https://www.organissimo.org/forum
- **JazzWax** — Marc Myers' daily jazz blog (since 2007); jazz history and interview
  pieces, organ sessions included. https://www.jazzwax.com
- **JazzFuel** — artist-management blog with jazz history and musician primers.
  https://www.jazzfuel.com
- **Vinyldiscovery** — blog (vinyldiscovery.blogspot.com) digging into rare groove and
  organ-led records. https://vinyldiscovery.blogspot.com
- **Sound On Sound** — UK recording magazine (web-first) with technical features on
  tonewheels, drawbars and Leslie simulation. https://www.soundonsound.com

## Use in this wiki

Forum threads and blog interviews support pages such as [[jimmy-mcgriff]],
[[dr-lonnie-smith]] and [[korg-cx-3]], and document the [[tonewheel-generator]] technology.
Ingest verbatim into `raw/articles/` (or `raw/forums/`) with provenance frontmatter.

- **TheatreOrgans.com Hammond FAQ** — the vintage-Hammond community's service-shop directory
  (Europe/Americas): dealer and technician entries, from the 1990s onward. Primary source for
  chop-shops and regional organ service history. https://theatreorgans.com/hammond/faq/
- **hammondjazznight.de** — Freiburg (Germany) event site documenting a "Bertram-Umbau"
  RT-3 with 22H Leslie (site parked at time of writing; text recovered from an indexed snippet).
  http://www.hammondjazznight.de/styled-3/index.html
