# Hammond & Organ YouTube Channels

Official and editorial YouTube channels documenting the Hammond organ: performances,
archival footage, tutorials and gear demos. Cite the URL and channel when ingesting.

## Channels

- **Hammond Organ Co.** — official channel of Hammond-Suzuki: product demos, artist
  features, history. https://www.youtube.com/@hammondorgan
- **Jazz Video Guy** — Bret Primack's channel (since 2006): jazz performance and
  documentary footage, organ trios included. https://www.youtube.com/@JazzVideoGuy
- **Blue Note** — official label channel with classic and modern organ-jazz performance
  clips. https://www.youtube.com/@BlueNote
- **Dr. Lonnie Smith (official)** — performance and interview footage of the organist
  documented at [[dr-lonnie-smith]]. https://www.youtube.com/@drlonniesmith
- **Tony Monaco (official)** — organist and educator: B-3 technique, drawbar settings,
  tips for players. https://www.youtube.com/@tonymonacob3
