# Suzuki Musical Instrument Corporation

Japanese musical-instrument conglomerate that acquired the [[hammond-organ-company|Hammond]] and [[donald-leslie|Leslie]] brands and now produces the "Hammond-Suzuki" line of digital clonewheels and modern Leslies.

## Hammond-Suzuki clonewheel lineage
- **XB-1, XB-2** (1991) — early modules / single-manual instruments.
- **XB-5** (1993), **XB-3** (1994), **XC-3** (1995) — mid-1990s consoles.
- **XM-1 / XMc-1** (1997), **XM-2 / XMc-2** (2006) — sound modules.
- **XK-2** (1999), **XK-3 / XLK-3** (2004), **XK-3c** (2008), **XK-1** (2006) — prosumer models.
- **New B-3 / New B-3 mk2 / XH-272 Elegante** — high-end consoles ($10,000+).
- **SK1 / SK2** (2011) — stage / home keyboards.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-clonewheel-organ.md]

Also the maker of the new line of Leslie speakers that ships with the XK-series organs.
