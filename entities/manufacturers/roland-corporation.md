# Roland Corporation

Japanese electronic-instrument maker with the longest-running third-party clonewheel line. Spawned from Ace Tone / Hammond joint ventures in Japan: the VK-9, VK-6, and VK-09 (c. 1978) were listed among "Hammond and related models," and the VK line continued for nearly forty years.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-clonewheel-organ.md]

## Roland clonewheel lineage
- **VK-9, VK-6, VK-09** (c. 1978) — Ace Tone-era spin-offs.
- **VK-1** (1980) — Roland first analog Hammond emulator.
- **Rhodes VK-1000** — modern digital variant.
- **VK-7** (1997) — wooden-case virtual-modeling flagship.
- **VK-77, VK-8, VK-8m, VK-88** (2002+) — iterations.
- **VR-760, VR-730, VR-09** (V-Combo) — combo-keyboard variants.
- **Atelier AT-900 / AT-900c / AT-800** — organ-focused lineup.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-clonewheel-organ.md]
