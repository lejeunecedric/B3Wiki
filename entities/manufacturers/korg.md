# Korg

Japanese electronic-instrument maker; producer of the [[korg-cx-3|CX-3]] (1980), the first major analog clonewheel that approached a real Hammond B-3 sound.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-clonewheel-organ.md]

## Clonewheel lineage
- **CX-3 / BX-3** (1980) — analog originals.
- **New CX-3 / New BX-3** (2000) — re-released in the modeling era.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-clonewheel-organ.md]
