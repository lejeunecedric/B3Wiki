# Crumar

**Crumar**, Italian manufacturer of electronic keyboards and combo organs (Padua; company founded 1969), one of the earliest names in the first wave of electronic attempts to copy the Hammond sound.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-clonewheel-organ.md]

## Products
- Early 1970s first-wave Hammond emulators: the **Crumar Organizer** console models ("Maharani", "Pergamon") and portable models (Haven, Organizer, Organizer2, Organizer T1/T2, Tokata), alongside the Roland VK-1/VK-9 and Yamaha YC45D.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hammond-organ.md]^[raw/articles/wikipedia-clonewheel-organ.md]
- Later clonewheel-era products: the VIP series ('70s), Professional 88 and 110 (1977/78), and the **DS-2** string synthesizer — see [[concepts/clonewheel-organ|clonewheel organ]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-clonewheel-organ.md]

## Place in the story
- The pre-digital emulation trail runs: Crumar Organizer (1970s) → [[manufacturers/korg|Korg]] CX-3 (1980, first analog clonewheel to truly approach a vintage [[hammond-b3|Hammond]]) → full virtual modeling ([[manufacturers/roland-corporation|Roland]] VK-7, [[manufacturers/clavia|Clavia]] Nord). See [[comparisons/b3-vs-clonewheels|B-3 vs clonewheels]].
