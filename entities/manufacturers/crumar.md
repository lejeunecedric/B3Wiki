# Crumar

**Crumar**, Italian manufacturer of electronic keyboards and combo organs (Padua; company founded 1969), one of the earliest names in the first wave of electronic attempts to copy the Hammond sound.[1]

## Products
- Early 1970s first-wave Hammond emulators: the **Crumar Organizer** console models ("Maharani", "Pergamon") and portable models (Haven, Organizer, Organizer2, Organizer T1/T2, Tokata), alongside the Roland VK-1/VK-9 and Yamaha YC45D.[2][1]
- Later clonewheel-era products: the VIP series ('70s), Professional 88 and 110 (1977/78), and the **DS-2** string synthesizer — see [clonewheel organ](/concepts/clonewheel-organ.md).[1]

## Place in the story
- The pre-digital emulation trail runs: Crumar Organizer (1970s) → [Korg](/entities/manufacturers/korg.md) CX-3 (1980, first analog clonewheel to truly approach a vintage [Hammond](/entities/instruments/hammond-b3.md)) → full virtual modeling ([Roland](/entities/manufacturers/roland-corporation.md) VK-7, [Clavia](/entities/manufacturers/clavia.md) Nord). See [B-3 vs clonewheels](/comparisons/b3-vs-clonewheels.md).
