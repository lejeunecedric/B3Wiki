# Clavia

Swedish electronic-instrument maker behind the **Nord** line of keyboards and combo organs. Maker of the [Nord C2D](/entities/instruments/nord-c2d.md) and the broader C Series, Nord Electro, and Nord Stage product lines.[1]

## Nord products
- **Nord Electro** series (2001-) — stage keyboard with organ section.
- **Nord Stage** series (2005-) — adds piano and synth.
- **Nord C1 / C2 / C2D** Combo Organ (2007-) — dedicated Hammond-style instrument with **button drawbars** (LEDs instead of sliders).
- **Nord Pedal Key 27** — MIDI pedalboard companion.[1]

Clavia is among the modern makers that publicly claim their modeling can recreate a vintage B-3 convincingly — though the debate over whether digital can really capture every nuance of a tonewheel organ remains open.[1]
