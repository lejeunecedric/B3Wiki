# Clavia

Swedish electronic-instrument maker behind the **Nord** line of keyboards and combo organs. Maker of the [[nord-c2d|Nord C2D]] and the broader C Series, Nord Electro, and Nord Stage product lines.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-clonewheel-organ.md]

## Nord products
- **Nord Electro** series (2001-) — stage keyboard with organ section.
- **Nord Stage** series (2005-) — adds piano and synth.
- **Nord C1 / C2 / C2D** Combo Organ (2007-) — dedicated Hammond-style instrument with **button drawbars** (LEDs instead of sliders).
- **Nord Pedal Key 27** — MIDI pedalboard companion.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-clonewheel-organ.md]

Clavia is among the modern makers that publicly claim their modeling can recreate a vintage B-3 convincingly — though the debate over whether digital can really capture every nuance of a tonewheel organ remains open.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-clonewheel-organ.md]
