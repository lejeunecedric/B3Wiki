# Manufacturers Index

> Auto-maintained list of pages in this folder. Main catalog: [[../index|index.md]].

- [[hammond-organ-company|Hammond Organ Company]] — Chicago company (1928-1985) that built ~2 million organs; brand now owned by Suzuki.
- [[suzuki-musical-instrument|Suzuki Musical Instrument Corporation]] — Japanese conglomerate that acquired the Hammond and Leslie brands and now produces the Hammond-Suzuki line.
- [[korg|Korg]] — Maker of the 1980 CX-3 / BX-3 analog clonewheels (and, since 1992, of Vox).
- [[roland-corporation|Roland Corporation]] — Maker of the VK-1, VK-7, VK-8 lineage of clonewheels (1978 onward).
- [[clavia|Clavia]] — Swedish maker of the Nord line of stage keyboards and combo organs.
- [[farfisa|Farfisa]] — Italian maker of cheap transistor combo organs (Compact, 1964); the B-3's portability/price rival.
- [[vox|Vox]] — British amp maker (1957); its 1962 Continental drawbar combo organ is the classic transistor rival.
- [[rmi|RMI (Rocky Mount Instruments)]] — Allen Organ subsidiary (1966-1982) making the Electra-pianos in Jon Lord's and Brian Auger's rigs.
- [[marshall-amplification|Marshall Amplification]] — British amp maker (1962); the overdriven stacks Jon Lord used to make the Hammond heavy.
