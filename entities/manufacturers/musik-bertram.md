# Musik-Bertram

German Hammond dealer and chop-shop based in **Freiburg im Breisgau** (postal address
D-79011 Freiburg, Postfach 1153; tel +49 (761) 27-30-90). Listed on the TheatreOrgans.com
Hammond FAQ Europe service-shops page (entry last updated Dec 1994) as "Hammond dealer,
builder of MIDI-Kit for Bass Pedals".^[raw/articles/theatreorgans-europe-shops.md]

## The "Bertram-Umbau" (Bertram conversion)

From the 1960s onward the Musikhaus Bertram specialized in moving the innards of original
wooden-console Hammonds (B-3, C-3, RT-3, A-100) into a purpose-built **transportable case**.
With dismountable bass pedals the converted organ weighs roughly **100 kg, versus ~250 kg
for the original console** — the portability problem that chopping solves. A Hammond RT-3
(c. 1961) in "Bertram-Umbau", with a two-octave bass pedal and an original 22H Leslie (1953),
has been heard at the Freiburg-based Hammond Jazz Night event.^[raw/articles/hammondjazznight-bertram-umbau.md]

Second-hand classified listings (Facebook Hammond groups) document A-100 consoles chopped to
220 V / 50 Hz by Bertram music — the 220 V/50 Hz adaptation being the European counterpart of
the American chop tradition.^[raw/articles/doctormix-rarest-hammond.md]

## Relationship to the chop tradition

Bertram-Umbau is the German branch of the broader **chop** practice documented in
[[portable-hammonds]] — alongside the Oakland chop tradition and US shops like Keyboard
Specialties. Where a chop typically cuts the console down to a two-manual top, the Bertram
conversion kept the full instrument but re-cased it for transport — related to the
[[tonewheel-generator]] innards it preserves.
