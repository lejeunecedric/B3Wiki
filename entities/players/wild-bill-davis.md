# Wild Bill Davis

American jazz organist — important bridge figure in the chain that runs from Fats Waller and Count Basie to [[jimmy-smith|Jimmy Smith]] and the modern jazz-organ trio tradition.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-jimmy-smith.md]

## Why he matters here
- Part of the gospel-organ to jazz-organ pipeline that grew out of Chicago First Church of Deliverance tradition.^[raw/articles/hammondorganco-hammond-story.md]
- Hearing him play in 1954 was the catalyst that made [[jimmy-smith|Jimmy Smith]] switch from piano to organ permanently.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-jimmy-smith.md]
