# Paolo "Apollo" Negri (b. 1979)

Italian Hammond organist, keyboardist and songwriter from Piacenza — the organist and principal songwriter of [[link-quartet|The Link Quartet]] since 1997 and the most prolific solo artist on the [[hammondbeat|Hammondbeat]] roster (four catalog albums under his own name).^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md]^[raw/articles/wikipedia-hammondbeat.md]

## Career
- Born 1979; at 16 founded his first band, the psychedelic-prog trio **Nice Price** (influences: The Nice, Quatermass, Arthur Brown). Joined The Link Quartet in 1997 after it split.^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md]
- His style "reminds Brian Auger, The Mohawks, and in a certain way Booker T, but keeps always a strong personality" (IAJO); [[booker-t-jones|Booker T]]-style groove with [[brian-auger|Auger]]-style aggression.^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md]
- With Link Quartet: support tours for [[james-taylor-quartet|JTQ]], Brian Auger's Oblivion Express, Manu Chao; albums *Episode 1*, *Beat.it*, *4*, *Hotel Constellation*.^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md]
- Central member and songwriter of the hard-prog band **Wicked Minds** (2000–2013). Session work on 300+ albums; also played with Black Widow, Ray Daytona & Googoobombos, Low Fidelity Jet-Set Orchestra, Fred Leslie's Missing Link.^[raw/articles/linkquartet-lastfm-apollonegri.md]

## Hammondbeat solo discography
- *A Bigger Tomorrow* (HBR-009, 2007) — a collaborative album inviting 20+ guests from the worldwide Hammond community (New Mastersounds, Diplomats of Solid Sound, Phat Fred, Nick Rossi Set members…), recorded on Hammond A-102 with pedalboard, Rhodes, synths and Mellotron
- *The Great Anything* (HBR-012, 2010)
- *COBOL* (HBR-014, 2011)
- *Hello World* (HBR-017, 2014 — the label's last main-catalog CD)

Within the revival scene he is the clearest through-line from the [[jimmy-smith]]-derived organ trio language into 2000s European club music — like [[eddy-louiss|Eddy Louiss]] in France, a national scene's reference organist.
