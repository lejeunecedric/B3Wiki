# Don Patterson

**Don Patterson** (1932–1988, dates pending a dedicated source), American soul-jazz organist of the post-[Jimmy Smith](/entities/players/jimmy-smith.md) school, a Prestige-label mainstay.[^wikipedia-jimmy-smith]

## Why he matters
- Named by [Jimmy Smith](/entities/players/jimmy-smith.md)'s Wikipedia article among the jazz organists in the Smith lineage.[^wikipedia-jimmy-smith]
- Named by Hammond Organ Co.'s history in the swinging-Sixties soul-jazz legion alongside [Jimmy McGriff](/entities/players/jimmy-mcgriff.md), "Brother" [Jack McDuff](/entities/players/jack-mcduff.md) and "Groove" Holmes.[^hammondorganco-hammond-story]
- The Vinyldiscovery soul-jazz survey lists him among the "Jimmy Smith disciples" organists who defined the bluesy 1960s style.[^vinyldiscovery-organ-jazz-soul-jazz]
- The organissimo forum's Blue-Note-Turns-66 thread highlights the **Don Patterson Trio** — Patterson (organ), Jerry Byrd (guitar), Billy James (drums) — with _Goin' to Meeting_ and _Satisfaction!_ (Prestige, recorded 19 July 1965 at Rudy Van Gelder's Studio) as representative sessions.[^organissimo-bft66-hammond-b3]
