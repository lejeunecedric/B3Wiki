# Don Patterson

**Don Patterson** (1932–1988, dates pending a dedicated source), American soul-jazz organist of the post-[[jimmy-smith|Jimmy Smith]] school, a Prestige-label mainstay.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-jimmy-smith.md]

## Why he matters
- Named by [[jimmy-smith|Jimmy Smith]]'s Wikipedia article among the jazz organists in the Smith lineage.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-jimmy-smith.md]
- Named by Hammond Organ Co.'s history in the swinging-Sixties soul-jazz legion alongside [[jimmy-mcgriff|Jimmy McGriff]], "Brother" [[jack-mcduff|Jack McDuff]] and "Groove" Holmes.^[raw/articles/hammondorganco-hammond-story.md]
- The Vinyldiscovery soul-jazz survey lists him among the "Jimmy Smith disciples" organists who defined the bluesy 1960s style.^[raw/articles/vinyldiscovery-organ-jazz-soul-jazz.md]
- The organissimo forum's Blue-Note-Turns-66 thread highlights the **Don Patterson Trio** — Patterson (organ), Jerry Byrd (guitar), Billy James (drums) — with _Goin' to Meeting_ and _Satisfaction!_ (Prestige, recorded 19 July 1965 at Rudy Van Gelder's Studio) as representative sessions.^[raw/articles/organissimo-bft66-hammond-b3.md]
