# Jimmy James (Jimmy James Williams)

Seattle guitarist (Jimmy James Williams) best known for his seven-year stint (2015–2022) as the guitarist of the [[delvon-lamarr-organ-trio]], where his blend of Steve Cropper chanking, Jimi Hendrix freak-outs and Grant Green-style jazz lines was half of the band's signature sound. He co-founded Seattle's nine-piece soul band True Loves with drummer David McGraw.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-delvon-lamarr-organ-trio.md]

After leaving DLO3 he fronted his own organ trio, [[parlor-greens]] (with Adam Scone on Hammond and Tim Carman on drums), formed in 2025 on Colemine Records — this is the "new Jimmy James trio" (debut album *Emeralds*, 2025). He also appears as a guest on Kendra Morris's Colemine album *Next* (2025).^[raw/articles/bandcampdaily-parlor-greens-emeralds.md]
