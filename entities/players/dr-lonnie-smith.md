# Dr. Lonnie Smith (1942–2021)

**Lonnie Smith** (July 3, 1942 – September 28, 2021), styled **Dr. Lonnie Smith**, American jazz, soul-jazz and funk [[hammond-b3|Hammond B-3]] organist. Member of the George Benson Quartet in the 1960s, Blue Note solo artist, and later owner of his own label, Pilgrimage. Named organist of the year by the Jazz Journalists Association nine times.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-dr-lonnie-smith.md]

## Early years
- Born in Lackawanna, New York; raised in a family vocal group with a radio program — gospel, classical and jazz all came through his mother.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-dr-lonnie-smith.md]
- Sang in 1950s vocal ensembles including the Teen Kings (with Grover Washington Jr.); Art Kubera, a local music-store owner, gave Smith his first organ — a [[hammond-b3|Hammond B-3]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-dr-lonnie-smith.md]

## Career milestones
- 1965: moved to New York City; 1966: formed the George Benson Quartet, featuring Lonnie Smith, after meeting Benson (ex-Jack McDuff band). Two Benson-led albums, _It's Uptown_ and _Cookbook_.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-dr-lonnie-smith.md]
- 1967: first solo album, _Finger Lickin' Good Soul Organ_ (Benson and Melvin Sparks on guitar); appeared on Lou Donaldson's Blue Note hit _Alligator Bogaloo_.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-dr-lonnie-smith.md]
- 1968: signed by Blue Note; five albums including _Think!_ and _Turning Point_ (both with Lee Morgan and Melvin Sparks), the live _Move Your Hand_ (1969) and _Live at Club Mozambique_ (1970).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-dr-lonnie-smith.md]
- 1971: one album for CTI's Kudu subsidiary; mid-1970s with producer Sonny Lester's Groove Merchant/LRC labels, moving through jazz, soul, funk, fusion and disco.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-dr-lonnie-smith.md]
- Over 30 albums under his own name; sidemen ranged from Lee Morgan and King Curtis to Joey DeFrancesco and Joe Lovano.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-dr-lonnie-smith.md]
- 2015: rejoined Blue Note after 45 years — _Evolution_ (2016), _All in My Mind_ (2018), and _Breathe_ (2021) with guest vocals from Iggy Pop.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-dr-lonnie-smith.md]
