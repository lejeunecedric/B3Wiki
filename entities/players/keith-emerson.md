# Keith Emerson (1944–2016)

**Keith Noel Emerson** (2 November 1944, Todmorden, Yorkshire – 11 March 2016, Santa Monica, California), English keyboardist and composer, co-founder of The Nice and of [Emerson, Lake & Palmer](/entities/albums/brain-salad-surgery.md) (ELP). One of the first rock keyboardists to treat the [Hammond organ](/entities/instruments/hammond-b3.md) as a full lead instrument — flipped, abused and pyrotechnic — alongside the Moog synthesizer.[^wikipedia-keith-emerson]

## The Hammond story
- Converted to the organ after hearing jazz organist [Jack McDuff](/entities/players/jack-mcduff.md) perform "Rock Candy"; the Hammond became his instrument of choice from the late 1960s.[^wikipedia-keith-emerson]
- His first Hammond was an **[L-100](/entities/instruments/hammond-l-100.md)** model, bought at age 15–16 on hire purchase plus a loan from his father — an instrument he famously abused on stage (knives in the keys, feedback, dragging), with the modded "wailing" switch trick and a burned-out survivor now at EMEAPP.[^wikipedia-keith-emerson][^musicradar-emerson-rig]
- In his Hammond playing he partly recreated the sound of jazz-blues organists; the classic [Hammond](/entities/instruments/hammond-b3.md) + [Leslie](/entities/instruments/leslie-speaker.md) pairing sat at the center of The Nice's and ELP's sound, with the L-100/C-3 played hard through overdriven amps.[^wikipedia-keith-emerson][^hammondorganco-hammond-story]

## Career milestones
- The Nice (1967–1970): R&B/classical hybrid built on the Hammond; "Rondo" and "America" as organ-led showpieces.
- Emerson, Lake & Palmer (1970–1979, reunions 1991–1998): _Brain Salad Surgery_ (1973) is the landmark [Hammond](/entities/instruments/hammond-b3.md)-and-Moog record — see [Brain Salad Surgery](/entities/albums/brain-salad-surgery.md).
- Both Emerson and Greg Lake died in 2016, leaving Carl Palmer as ELP's only surviving member.[^wikipedia-keith-emerson]
- His full Hammond rig (Tarkus C-3, L-100s, twin Leslie 122s) is preserved at EMEAPP; organ tech Al Goff documented the mods.[^musicradar-emerson-rig]
