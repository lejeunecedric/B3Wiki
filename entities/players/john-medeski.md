# John Medeski (b. 1965)

**Anthony John Medeski** (born 28 June 1965, Louisville, Kentucky) is an American jazz keyboardist and composer, veteran of New York's 1990s avant-garde jazz scene and best known as a member of **Medeski Martin & Wood** (MMW, formed New York 1991 with Billy Martin and Chris Wood).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-john-medeski.md]

## The Hammond in his toolkit
Medeski plays an unusually eclectic array of keyboards — acoustic piano (a Steinway Artist), melodica, mellotron, clavinet, Wurlitzer, Moog Voyager, Vox Continental — but the [[hammond-b3|Hammond B-3]] organ is the anchor voice of MMW's groove-based records and of his side projects.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-john-medeski.md] His organ work spans:

- **MMW** — steady international touring through funk, jazz and jam scenes since the mid-1990s; albums on Gramavision and Blue Note (_Shack-man_, _Combustication_, _The Dropper_).
- **With John Scofield** — _A Go Go_ (1998) and the Medeski Scofield Martin & Wood albums (_Out Louder_, 2006; _Juice_, 2014).
- **The Word** (2000) — a blues-gospel project with Robert Randolph and the Dickinson brothers, putting the B-3 back in sacred-adjacent territory.
- **John Medeski & The Itch** (2006, with Eric Krasno and Adam Deitch) — billed explicitly around his B-3 playing.
- Session work: Morphine's _The Night_ (1999), the Dirty Dozen Brass Band's _Buck Jump_ (1999), the Bob Dylan biopic soundtrack _I'm Not There_ (2006), Coheed and Cambria's _The Afterman_ albums (2012).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-john-medeski.md]

## Why he matters to this wiki
Medeski is the clearest case of the [[hammond-b3|B-3]] surviving inside the post-[[clonewheel-organ|1990s jam/acid-jazz]] economy: a working organist whose sound is built on vintage drawbar language (see [[drawbars]]) yet whose scene grew up alongside digital emulations. He bridges the soul-jazz lineage documented in [[jimmy-smith]] and the revival infrastructure of [[hammondbeat]].
