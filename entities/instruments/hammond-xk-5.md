# Hammond-Suzuki XK-5 (2016)

Flagship portable [clonewheel](/concepts/clonewheel-organ.md) from [Hammond-Suzuki](/entities/manufacturers/suzuki-musical-instrument.md), launched in late 2016 (Hugh Robjohns' January 2018 *Sound on Sound* review calls it "a little over a year" old) as an entirely new design superseding the [XK-3/XK-3C](/entities/instruments/hammond-xk-3.md) — which was discontinued with its arrival.[^sos-hammond-xk-5-2018]

## What's new vs the XK-3C

- **MTW I sound engine** ("Modelled ToneWheel One") — combines sampling and modelling where the older XK/SK ranges used the VASE III engine from the New B-3. 96 virtual tonewheels, each individually adjustable (level, high-pass filtering, leakage level and even which wheel dominates leakage). Four sampled generator sets (A100, C3, B3, "Mellow") each with three variations plus three user slots; the manual even lists the serial numbers of the sampled source instruments.[^sos-hammond-xk-5-2018]
- **Bespoke keybed** — built in-house in Japan (previous keybeds were bought-in OEM units), with a patented three-contact system that emulates the vintage nine-busbar feel: each mechanical contact triggers three virtual "note-on" signals so all nine drawbar pitches start at slightly different, user-configurable times. Reviewer Hugh Robjohns found it "almost indistinguishable" from his own 1961 [A-100](/entities/instruments/hammond-b3.md)-class console.[^sos-hammond-xk-5-2018]
- **Full drawbar complement** — two complete sets per manual plus a pedal pair, a first for a portable Hammond.[^sos-hammond-xk-5-2018]
- **Matching-transformer modelling** — replicates magnetic saturation and hysteresis, i.e. the "volume robbing" that makes a real Hammond's dynamics depend on what was played before.[^sos-hammond-xk-5-2018]
- **Vibrato scanner eras** — options replicating the scanner delay-line designs of 1955–57, 1957–59 and 1959–74, where the XK/SK generations model only one type.[^sos-hammond-xk-5-2018]
- **Tube preamp** — physical 12AX7 and 12AU7 valves, configurable and swappable in the signal chain.[^sos-hammond-xk-5-2018]
- **USB audio/MIDI** — class-compliant USB-to-host carrying MIDI and stereo audio both ways.[^sos-hammond-xk-5-2018]

## Expansion and firmware

Ships as a single 73-note manual (16 kg, same footprint as the XK-3C) expandable into the "XK-5 Heritage Pro System" with the XLK-5 lower manual, folding frame and pedalboard; in the US the spindle-legged traditional variant was marketed as the "Model A-3 System". At launch the engine was pure console Hammond with no extra voices; firmware 3.0 (April 2018) ported the Vox, Farfisa and pipe-organ emulations across.[^sos-hammond-xk-5-2018]

## Why it matters

The XK-5 is the clearest evidence that the clonewheel race moved from *sound* to *touch*: after a decade in which digital engines had largely matched the tonewheel sound (see [B-3 vs Clonewheels](/comparisons/b3-vs-clonewheels.md)), Hammond-Suzuki's remaining competitive gap was keybed feel and playing response — and the XK-5 attacked exactly that, winning an instant market success.[^sos-hammond-xk-5-2018]
