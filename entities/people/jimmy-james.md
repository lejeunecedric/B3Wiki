# Jimmy James (Jimmy James Williams)

> **Note:** Jimmy James is a **guitarist**, not an organist — he lives in `entities/people/` because he is a performing musician central to the organ-trio story (Delvon Lamarr OT, Parlor Greens), but does not belong in `players/` (organists only, per AGENTS.md §7).

Seattle guitarist (Jimmy James Williams) best known for his seven-year stint (2015–2022) as the guitarist of the [Delvon Lamarr Organ Trio](/entities/bands/delvon-lamarr-organ-trio.md), where his blend of Steve Cropper chanking, Jimi Hendrix freak-outs and Grant Green-style jazz lines was half of the band's signature sound. He co-founded Seattle's nine-piece soul band True Loves with drummer David McGraw.[1]

After leaving DLO3 he fronted his own organ trio, [Parlor Greens](/entities/bands/parlor-greens.md) (with Adam Scone on Hammond and Tim Carman on drums), formed in 2025 on Colemine Records — this is the "new Jimmy James trio" (debut album *Emeralds*, 2025). He also appears as a guest on Kendra Morris's Colemine album *Next* (2025).[2]
