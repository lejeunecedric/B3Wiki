# Mark Naftalin

**Mark Naftalin**, American keyboardist — pianist/organist/keyboards man in the 1968 lineup of [Mother Earth](/entities/bands/mother-earth.md) (US), formerly of the Paul Butterfield Blues Band. **Note: a multi-keyboardist rather than a dedicated organist**, hence filed here rather than in players/.[1][2]

## The Hammond connection
- Credited with piano, organ and keyboards on _[Living with the Animals](/entities/albums/living-with-the-animals.md)_ (1968) — the blues-rock organ backing behind Tracy Nelson's vocals.[2]
- The exact organ chair on each track (Naftalin vs. session player Barry Goldberg) rests on the album's liner credits as relayed by a collector blog — hence `confidence: medium`. See [landmark Hammond albums query](/queries/hammond-landmark-albums.md).[2]
