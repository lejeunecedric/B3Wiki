# Mark Naftalin

**Mark Naftalin**, American keyboardist — pianist/organist/keyboards man in the 1968 lineup of [[bands/mother-earth|Mother Earth]] (US), formerly of the Paul Butterfield Blues Band. **Note: a multi-keyboardist rather than a dedicated organist**, hence filed here rather than in players/.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-mother-earth-american-band.md]^[raw/articles/rockasteria-mother-earth-living-with-animals.md]

## The Hammond connection
- Credited with piano, organ and keyboards on _[[albums/living-with-the-animals|Living with the Animals]]_ (1968) — the blues-rock organ backing behind Tracy Nelson's vocals.^[raw/articles/rockasteria-mother-earth-living-with-animals.md]
- The exact organ chair on each track (Naftalin vs. session player Barry Goldberg) rests on the album's liner credits as relayed by a collector blog — hence `confidence: medium`.
