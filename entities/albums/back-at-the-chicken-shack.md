# Back at the Chicken Shack

**Back at the Chicken Shack** is a [[jimmy-smith|Jimmy Smith]] album **recorded in 1960** and **released in 1963** on the **Blue Note** label, from the same sessions as *Midnight Special*; it was later cited in the book *1001 Albums You Must Hear Before You Die*.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-back-at-the-chicken-shack.md]

## The Hammond in it

The definitive Smith trio-plus format: Smith on organ with **Stanley Turrentine** (tenor sax), **Kenny Burrell** (guitar, tracks 1/4/5) and **Donald Bailey** (drums), produced by **Alfred Lion** and engineered by **Rudy Van Gelder**, with design by Reid Miles.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-back-at-the-chicken-shack.md]

Musically it is Smith's blues-drenched sideline to his bebop outings — walking bass pedals, drawbar growl and harmonic percussion in service of a slow blues title track that became one of his signature records, and a key document of the [[blue-note-records|Blue Note]] organ sound (see [[b3-beyond-jimmy-smith]]).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-jimmy-smith.md]
