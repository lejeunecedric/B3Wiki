# Living with the Animals

_Living with the Animals_ (1968, Mercury Records) — the debut album of the American blues-rock band [[bands/mother-earth|Mother Earth]], fronted by Tracy Nelson. A landmark of the blues-rock **Hammond-in-support** idiom: organ as pads, fills and groove behind a powerful blues voice rather than as a lead instrument.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-mother-earth-american-band.md]

## The organ role
- Often remembered as Nelson's vehicle, but a true group effort: tight R&B-derived playing, Mike Bloomfield's guest guitar, and sardonic Powell St. John songs ("Living with the Animals", "Marvel Set").^[raw/articles/rockasteria-mother-earth-living-with-animals.md]
- Keyboard chair: **[[mark-naftalin|Mark Naftalin]]** (ex-Paul Butterfield Blues Band) on piano, organ and keyboards, with session organ by Barry Goldberg — in the church-and-Chicago supporting tradition.^[raw/articles/rockasteria-mother-earth-living-with-animals.md]
- Listed in [[queries/hammond-landmark-albums|the landmark Hammond albums query]] as the blues-rock counterpoint to the lead-organ records of [[jon-lord|Jon Lord]] ([[albums/machine-head|Machine Head]]) and [[albums/abraxas|Gregg Rolie's Santana work]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-mother-earth-american-band.md]
