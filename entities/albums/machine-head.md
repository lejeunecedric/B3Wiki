# Machine Head

**Machine Head** is the sixth studio album by **Deep Purple** (Mark II), **released 30 March 1972**.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-machine-head-album.md] It is the band's best-selling record and one of hard rock's foundational albums.

## The Hammond in it

[[jon-lord|Jon Lord]]'s Hammond B-3/C-3 — driven into overdriven Marshall stacks and swirling Leslie — is the album's second lead instrument: rhythmic underpinning on "Smoke on the Water" and full organ solos on "Highway Star", "Pictures of Home" and "Lazy".^[raw/articles/wikipedia-machine-head-album.md]^[raw/articles/wikipedia-jon-lord.md] Lord later named his "Lazy" solo among his own favourites.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-jon-lord.md]

The album is the canonical proof that the tonewheel engine could carry hard rock's volume war: drawbar settings pushed to an aggressive reedy voice, percussion turned on, distortion treated as tone (see [[b3-vs-combo-organs]] for what the combo-organ rivals could not do here).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-jon-lord.md]
