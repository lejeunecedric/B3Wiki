# Abraxas

**Abraxas** is the second studio album by **Santana**, **released 23 September 1970**; a fusion of Latin rock, blues and jazz that topped the Billboard chart.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-abraxas-album.md]

## The Hammond in it

Founding keyboardist/vocalist **Gregg Rolie** plays the Hammond B-3 as the band's harmonic glue: organ pads and answer phrases under Carlos Santana's lead guitar on "Black Magic Woman" and "Oye Cómo Va", with the organ-vs-guitar call-and-response forming the album's core texture.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-abraxas-album.md] On "Incident at Neshabur" Rolie's keyboards are deliberately contrasted with guest pianist Alberto Gianquinto's jazz-influenced style.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-abraxas-album.md]

Rolie's approach — jazz-chord vocabulary inside percussion-driven grooves, the organ as a *conversational partner* rather than the solo star — extends the soul-jazz organ tradition into Latin rock (see [[hammond-landmark-albums]]).^[raw/articles/wikipedia-gregg-rolie.md]
