# I Told You So

**I Told You So** is the second studio album by the [Delvon Lamarr Organ Trio](/entities/bands/delvon-lamarr-organ-trio.md), **released January 29, 2021** on [Colemine Records](/entities/labels/colemine-records.md).[^wikipedia-delvon-lamarr-organ-trio]

## The Hammond in it

[Delvon Lamarr](/entities/players/delvon-lamarr.md)'s self-taught B-3 playing — punchy one-chord soul-jazz grooves in the [Jimmy Smith](/entities/players/jimmy-smith.md)/[McGriff](/entities/players/jimmy-mcgriff.md) lineage, played with a streaming-era warmth — made the album a Billboard-charting breakthrough for the modern organ-trio revival documented in [The modern organ scene](/queries/modern-organ-scene.md).[^wikipedia-delvon-lamarr-organ-trio] The trio at the time featured Jimmy James on guitar; the album's success carried the band from Seattle club act to international touring headliner.[^wikipedia-delvon-lamarr-organ-trio]

It is the clearest evidence that the classic organ-trio format still charts in the 2020s.[^colemine-records-about-us]
