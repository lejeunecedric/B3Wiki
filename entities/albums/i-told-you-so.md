# I Told You So

**I Told You So** is the second studio album by the [[delvon-lamarr-organ-trio|Delvon Lamarr Organ Trio]], **released January 29, 2021** on [[colemine-records|Colemine Records]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-delvon-lamarr-organ-trio.md]

## The Hammond in it

[[delvon-lamarr|Delvon Lamarr]]'s self-taught B-3 playing — punchy one-chord soul-jazz grooves in the [[jimmy-smith|Jimmy Smith]]/[[jimmy-mcgriff|McGriff]] lineage, played with a streaming-era warmth — made the album a Billboard-charting breakthrough for the modern organ-trio revival documented in [[modern-organ-scene|The modern organ scene]].^[raw/articles/wikipedia-delvon-lamarr-organ-trio.md] The trio at the time featured Jimmy James on guitar; the album's success carried the band from Seattle club act to international touring headliner.^[raw/articles/wikipedia-delvon-lamarr-organ-trio.md]

It is the clearest evidence that the classic organ-trio format still charts in the 2020s.^[raw/articles/colemine-records-about-us.md]
