# Brain Salad Surgery

**Brain Salad Surgery** is the fourth studio album by **Emerson, Lake & Palmer**, **released 7 December 1973** on their own Manticore Records label, distributed by Atlantic.[1]

## The Hammond in it

'Keith Emerson''s keyboard arsenal on the album pairs the Moog synthesizer with his abused Hammond: the three-movement sci-fi suite **"Karn Evil 9"** makes the Hammond and Moog equal lead voices, and the record also carries his adaptation of Ginastera's 1st Piano Concerto (the notorious "Toccata").[1][2]

Emerson's Hammond technique — coming from the jazz-blues organ records (Jack McDuff's "Rock Candy") and expressed on an L-100 he treated with knives and stunts — here sits inside the largest canvas the prog era gave the instrument.[3] It closes the trilogy of prog Hammond landmarks begun with *Emerson, Lake & Palmer* (1970) and *Tarkus* (1971) (see [Landmark albums with great Hammond organ — Deep Purple, ELP, Mother Earth, Santana](/queries/hammond-landmark-albums.md)).
