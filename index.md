### Instruments
<!-- Hammond and third-party organs, speakers, gear-as-instrument. Clonewheels (Korg CX-3, Roland VK series...) also go here. -->

- [Hammond B-3](/entities/instruments/hammond-b3.md) - The iconic 1954-1975 console with harmonic percussion; the definitive jazz organ.
- [Leslie Speaker](/entities/instruments/leslie-speaker.md) - Rotating-horn/drum speaker creating the classic Doppler shimmer; models 122 & 147.
- [Korg CX-3](/entities/instruments/korg-cx-3.md) - 1980 single-manual analog clonewheel; the first clone to "approach a vintage Hammond."
- [Roland VK-7](/entities/instruments/roland-vk-7.md) - 1997 dual-manual virtual-modeling clonewheel in a wooden case.
- [Hammond-Suzuki XK-3](/entities/instruments/hammond-xk-3.md) - 2004 prosumer DSP clonewheel; third-generation virtual B-3.
- [Hammond XK-5](/entities/instruments/hammond-xk-5.md) - 2016 flagship portable clonewheel; MTW I engine, in-house keybed emulating the nine-busbar contact feel.
- [Clavia Nord C2D](/entities/instruments/nord-c2d.md) - 2012 dual-manual combo organ with LED-button drawbars.
- [Hammond L-100](/entities/instruments/hammond-l-100.md) - Economy spinet line (with M-100); Keith Emerson's first Hammond and knife-scarred stunt organ — the "wailing" switch trick, a burned Boston survivor at EMEAPP.

### Players

- [Jimmy Smith](/entities/players/jimmy-smith.md) — Jazz organist who defined the modern B-3 style and the organ trio format.
- [Jimmy McGriff](/entities/players/jimmy-mcgriff.md) — Philadelphia soul-jazz organist (1936–2008); blues/"fatback groove" counterpoint to Smith's bebop.
- [Dr. Lonnie Smith](/entities/players/dr-lonnie-smith.md) — B-3 organist (1942–2021) spanning soul-jazz, funk and a late-career Blue Note return.
- [Jon Lord](/entities/players/jon-lord.md) — Deep Purple co-founder whose classical-grounded Hammond playing drove hard rock and proto-metal.
- [Brian Auger](/entities/players/brian-auger.md) — British jazz-rock Hammond specialist; Steampacket, Trinity, Oblivion Express.
- [John Medeski](/entities/players/john-medeski.md) — MMW keyboardist (b. 1965); the B-3 anchor voice of the 1990s jam/acid-jazz scene.
- [Joey DeFrancesco](/entities/players/joey-defrancesco.md) — Jazz organist (1971–2022) who revived the B-3 in mainstream jazz; later acquired Jon Lord's modified Hammond.
- [Barbara Dennerlein](/entities/players/barbara-dennerlein.md) — German "Organ Tornado" (b. 1964); bass-pedal virtuoso with MIDI/sampler-modified Hammond.
- [Booker T. Jones](/entities/players/booker-t-jones.md) — Stax organist (b. 1944); "Green Onions" and the bridge from church/R&B organ to rock.
- [Eddy Louiss](/entities/players/eddy-louiss.md) — French jazz organist (1941–2015); Nougaro's accompanist, Stan Getz quartet 1971, France's beloved Hammond voice.
- [Paolo 'Apollo' Negri](/entities/players/paolo-negri.md) — Italian organist (b. 1979) of The Link Quartet; Hammondbeat's most prolific solo artist.
- [Delvon Lamarr](/entities/players/delvon-lamarr.md) — Self-taught Seattle organist; leader of DLO3, drummer-turned-organist with perfect pitch.
- [Jimmy James](/entities/people/jimmy-james.md) — Seattle **guitarist** (not an organist) of DLO3 (2015–2022), True Loves, and now Parlor Greens.
- [Cory Henry](/entities/players/cory-henry.md) — Brooklyn gospel-to-funk keyboardist (ex-Snarky Puppy); leads The Funk Apostles.
- [Keith Emerson](/entities/players/keith-emerson.md) — The Nice/ELP keyboardist (1944–2016) who made the abused Hammond a rock lead instrument; born of Jack McDuff's "Rock Candy".
- [Gregg Rolie](/entities/players/gregg-rolie.md) — Santana founder-vocalist (b. 1947); B-3 in call-and-response with Carlos Santana's guitar, later Journey.
- ["Brother" Jack McDuff](/entities/players/jack-mcduff.md) — Soul-jazz organist who switched from piano to Hammond in the 1950s; turned Keith Emerson onto the B-3.
- [Don Patterson](/entities/players/don-patterson.md) — Post-Smith Prestige-label soul-jazz organist; Don Patterson Trio, Satisfaction! (1965).
- [Richard "Groove" Holmes](/entities/players/richard-groove-holmes.md) — Soul-jazz organist who converted Jimmy McGriff to the organ; Groove Merchant veteran.
- [Charles Earland](/entities/players/charles-earland.md) — Philadelphia soul-jazz organist (1941–1999), "The Mighty Burner"; saxophonist-turned-organist from McGriff's combo, Prestige run and 1978 disco hit.
- [Larry Young](/entities/players/larry-young.md) — Newark modal innovator (1940–1978); the Coltrane-of-the-organ counter-branch of the soul-jazz legion, Blue Note's *Unity* and Tony Williams Lifetime.

### Bands
<!-- Organ-led bands (acid-jazz/revival scene and beyond). -->

- [The James Taylor Quartet](/entities/bands/james-taylor-quartet.md) — British acid-jazz band (1985–) around Hammond player James Taylor; the template for the modern organ revival.
- [The Link Quartet](/entities/bands/link-quartet.md) — Piacenza, Italy quartet (1993–); Hammondbeat's flagship act, JTQ-influenced funk/rock/soul.

- [Leslie Overdrive](/entities/bands/leslie-overdrive.md) — Danish organ-funk quartet (2005–); ...With the Hammond, In the Beauty (2007).
- [Delvon Lamarr Organ Trio](/entities/bands/delvon-lamarr-organ-trio.md) — Seattle soul-jazz trio (2015–); Colemine's Billboard-charting flagship of the modern revival.
- [Parlor Greens](/entities/bands/parlor-greens.md) — Jimmy James's 2025 organ trio with Adam Scone and Tim Carman (Colemine); Emeralds.
- [The Pulitzers](/entities/bands/the-pulitzers.md) — Dutch Hammond funk quartet (Frank Montis); Honk Honk (Zip Records, 2023).
- [Cookin' on 3 Burners](/entities/bands/cookin-on-3-burners.md) — Melbourne Hammond trio (1997–); "This Girl", the Kungs 2016 #1.
- [Mother Earth (US)](/entities/bands/mother-earth.md) — San Francisco blues-rock band (1967); Living with the Animals (1968), organ chair Naftalin/Goldberg.

### Labels

- [Hammondbeat Records](/entities/labels/hammondbeat.md) — Organ-sound record label (2002), grown from the JTQgroove acid-jazz fan site.
- [Blue Note Records](/entities/labels/blue-note-records.md) — Jazz label (1939); codified the soul-jazz organ with Jimmy Smith and Dr. Lonnie Smith.
- [Stax Records](/entities/labels/stax-records.md) — Memphis soul label (1957); Booker T. Jones's B-3 turned church organ into hit R&B.
- [Verve Records](/entities/labels/verve-records.md) — Norman Granz's jazz label (1956); carried Jimmy Smith to mass popularity after Blue Note.
- [Colemine Records](/entities/labels/colemine-records.md) — Ohio soul/funk indie (2007, incl. Karma Chief); flagship label of the modern organ-trio revival.
- [Groove Merchant](/entities/labels/groove-merchant.md) — Sonny Lester's 1970s soul-jazz label; second act for McGriff and Lonnie Smith.
- [Zip Records](/entities/labels/zip-records.md) — San Francisco/Amsterdam indie (1997); home of The Pulitzers' Hammond funk.
- [Wild Bill Davis](/entities/players/wild-bill-davis.md) - Bridge figure in the gospel-to-jazz-organ lineage; the catalyst for Jimmy Smith's switch to organ.

### Manufacturers
<!-- Companies building organs/clone organs (Hammond, Suzuki, Korg, Roland, Clavia...). -->

- [Musik-Bertram](/entities/manufacturers/musik-bertram.md) - Freiburg Hammond dealer/chop-shop (from the 1960s): ~100 kg transportable "Bertram-Umbau" re-casings, 220 V/50 Hz chops, MIDI bass-pedal kit.
- [Hammond Organ Company](/entities/manufacturers/hammond-organ-company.md) - Chicago company (1928-1985) that built ~2 million organs; brand now owned by Suzuki.
- [Suzuki Musical Instrument Corporation](/entities/manufacturers/suzuki-musical-instrument.md) - Japanese conglomerate that now owns and produces the Hammond + Leslie lines.
- [Korg](/entities/manufacturers/korg.md) - Maker of the 1980 CX-3 / BX-3 analog clonewheels.
- [Roland Corporation](/entities/manufacturers/roland-corporation.md) - Maker of the VK-1, VK-7, VK-8 lineage of clonewheels (1978 onward).
- [Clavia](/entities/manufacturers/clavia.md) - Swedish maker of the Nord line of stage keyboards and combo organs.
- [Farfisa](/entities/manufacturers/farfisa.md) - Italian maker of cheap transistor combo organs (Compact, 1964); the B-3's portability/price rival.
- [Vox](/entities/manufacturers/vox.md) - British amp maker (1957); its 1962 Continental drawbar combo organ is the classic transistor rival.
- [RMI (Rocky Mount Instruments)](/entities/manufacturers/rmi.md) - Allen Organ subsidiary (1966-1982) making the Electra-pianos in Jon Lord's and Brian Auger's rigs.
- [Marshall Amplification](/entities/manufacturers/marshall-amplification.md) - British amp maker (1962); the overdriven stacks Jon Lord used to make the Hammond heavy.
- [Crumar](/entities/manufacturers/crumar.md) - Italian maker (1969) of the Organizer first-wave Hammond emulators and later clonewheel-era boards.

## Concepts

- [Tonewheel Generator](/concepts/tonewheel-generator.md) - Rotating metal wheels + magnetic pickups producing additive tones, driven by a synchronous motor.
- [Drawbars & Additive Synthesis](/concepts/drawbars.md) - Sliders mixing pipe-organ footages (16'-1') to shape timbre in real time.
- [Harmonic Percussion](/concepts/harmonic-percussion.md) - B-3's decaying 2nd/3rd-harmonic "click" on the upper manual; the soul of the jazz sound.
- [Vibrato/Chorus Scanner](/concepts/vibrato-chorus.md) - Delay-line scanner giving the Hammond its signature shimmer (C-3/V/CV models onward).
- [Clonewheel Organ](/concepts/clonewheel-organ.md) - Electronic instruments that emulate the tonewheel-organ sound without actual spinning wheels.
- [Portable Hammonds](/concepts/portable-hammonds.md) - Factory portables (B-3mk2, Porta-B) vs chops (Keyboard Specialties, Bertram music); the 50/60 Hz problem.
