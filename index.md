### Instruments

- [[instruments/hammond-b3|Hammond B-3]] - The iconic 1954-1975 console with harmonic percussion; the definitive jazz organ.
- [[instruments/leslie-speaker|Leslie Speaker]] - Rotating-horn/drum speaker creating the classic Doppler shimmer; models 122 & 147.
- [[instruments/korg-cx-3|Korg CX-3]] - 1980 single-manual analog clonewheel; the first clone to "approach a vintage Hammond."
- [[instruments/roland-vk-7|Roland VK-7]] - 1997 dual-manual virtual-modeling clonewheel in a wooden case.
- [[instruments/hammond-xk-3|Hammond-Suzuki XK-3]] - 2004 prosumer DSP clonewheel; third-generation virtual B-3.
- [[instruments/nord-c2d|Clavia Nord C2D]] - 2012 dual-manual combo organ with LED-button drawbars.

### Players

- [[players/jimmy-smith|Jimmy Smith]] — Jazz organist who defined the modern B-3 style and the organ trio format.
- [[players/jimmy-mcgriff|Jimmy McGriff]] — Philadelphia soul-jazz organist (1936–2008); blues/"fatback groove" counterpoint to Smith's bebop.
- [[players/dr-lonnie-smith|Dr. Lonnie Smith]] — B-3 organist (1942–2021) spanning soul-jazz, funk and a late-career Blue Note return.
- [[players/jon-lord|Jon Lord]] — Deep Purple co-founder whose classical-grounded Hammond playing drove hard rock and proto-metal.
- [[players/brian-auger|Brian Auger]] — British jazz-rock Hammond specialist; Steampacket, Trinity, Oblivion Express.
- [[players/john-medeski|John Medeski]] — MMW keyboardist (b. 1965); the B-3 anchor voice of the 1990s jam/acid-jazz scene.
- [[players/joey-defrancesco|Joey DeFrancesco]] — Jazz organist (1971–2022) who revived the B-3 in mainstream jazz; later acquired Jon Lord's modified Hammond.
- [[players/barbara-dennerlein|Barbara Dennerlein]] — German "Organ Tornado" (b. 1964); bass-pedal virtuoso with MIDI/sampler-modified Hammond.
- [[players/booker-t-jones|Booker T. Jones]] — Stax organist (b. 1944); "Green Onions" and the bridge from church/R&B organ to rock.
- [[players/eddy-louiss|Eddy Louiss]] — French jazz organist (1941–2015); Nougaro's accompanist, Stan Getz quartet 1971, France's beloved Hammond voice.
- [[players/paolo-negri|Paolo 'Apollo' Negri]] — Italian organist (b. 1979) of The Link Quartet; Hammondbeat's most prolific solo artist.

### Bands

- [[bands/link-quartet|The Link Quartet]] — Piacenza, Italy quartet (1993–); Hammondbeat's flagship act, JTQ-influenced funk/rock/soul.

- [[bands/leslie-overdrive|Leslie Overdrive]] — Danish organ-funk quartet (2005–); ...With the Hammond, In the Beauty (2007).

### Labels

- [[labels/hammondbeat|Hammondbeat Records]] — Organ-sound record label (2002), grown from the JTQgroove acid-jazz fan site.
- [[players/wild-bill-davis|Wild Bill Davis]] - Bridge figure in the gospel-to-jazz-organ lineage; the catalyst for Jimmy Smith's switch to organ.

### Manufacturers

- [[manufacturers/hammond-organ-company|Hammond Organ Company]] - Chicago company (1928-1985) that built ~2 million organs; brand now owned by Suzuki.
- [[manufacturers/suzuki-musical-instrument|Suzuki Musical Instrument Corporation]] - Japanese conglomerate that now owns and produces the Hammond + Leslie lines.
- [[manufacturers/korg|Korg]] - Maker of the 1980 CX-3 / BX-3 analog clonewheels.
- [[manufacturers/roland-corporation|Roland Corporation]] - Maker of the VK-1, VK-7, VK-8 lineage of clonewheels (1978 onward).
- [[manufacturers/clavia|Clavia]] - Swedish maker of the Nord line of stage keyboards and combo organs.

### People
<!-- Inventors, engineers, non-player figures. -->

- [[people/laurens-hammond|Laurens Hammond]] - Mechanical engineer/inventor (clocks, 3D glasses) who created the tonewheel organ in 1935.
- [[people/donald-leslie|Donald J. Leslie]] - Engineer who invented the rotating Leslie speaker; Hammond rejected it, history adopted it.
- [[people/renato-lombardi|Renato Lombardi]] - Italian amp/sound engineer behind Brian Auger's signature sound (resistor fix, free PA stacks).

## Concepts

- [[concepts/tonewheel-generator|Tonewheel Generator]] - Rotating metal wheels + magnetic pickups producing additive tones, driven by a synchronous motor.
- [[concepts/drawbars|Drawbars & Additive Synthesis]] - Sliders mixing pipe-organ footages (16'-1') to shape timbre in real time.
- [[concepts/harmonic-percussion|Harmonic Percussion]] - B-3's decaying 2nd/3rd-harmonic "click" on the upper manual; the soul of the jazz sound.
- [[concepts/vibrato-chorus|Vibrato/Chorus Scanner]] - Delay-line scanner giving the Hammond its signature shimmer (C-3/V/CV models onward).
- [[concepts/clonewheel-organ|Clonewheel Organ]] - Electronic instruments that emulate the tonewheel-organ sound without actual spinning wheels.
- [[concepts/portable-hammonds|Portable Hammonds]] - Factory portables (B-3mk2, Porta-B) vs chops (Keyboard Specialties, Bertram music); the 50/60 Hz problem.

## Comparisons

- [[comparisons/b3-vs-a100|B-3 vs A-100]] - The 1959 self-contained B-3 with built-in amp and speakers — identical organ engine, simpler rig.
- [[comparisons/b3-vs-c3|B-3 vs C-3]] - Same tonewheel generator, different cabinet: B-3 open "showbiz" vs C-3 church-style with modesty panel.
- [[comparisons/b3-vs-combo-organs|B-3 vs Combo Organs]] - Tonewheel heavyweight vs transistor combo rivals (Vox Continental, Farfisa): portability and price vs depth of tone.
- [[comparisons/b3-vs-clonewheels|B-3 vs Clonewheels]] - Vintage B-3 vs CX-3/VK-7/Nord C/XK-3 digital emulations: what modeling caught up on, and what it can't (key feel, leakage, real Leslie).
