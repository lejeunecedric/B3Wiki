Fresh finds in this week's music papers — all due Friday, October 23, on
Colemine Records, the Loveland, Ohio soul stable:

**Surprise Chef — Frogs In A Pond** (LP, out Friday) — the Melbourne
instrumental crew returns with a new LP of warm, organ-led groove-funk.
([Colemine](https://www.coleminerecords.com/))

**The Charities — Movin' On / I'm Still Learning How** (7", out Friday) —
two new sides of sweet, horn-backed soul from the Cali ensemble.
([Colemine](https://www.coleminerecords.com/))

**Adebowale and the Pioneers — Kilofese** (out Friday) — the Chicago
afrobeat drummer's latest for Colemine's friends-of-the-label shelf.
([Colemine](https://www.coleminerecords.com/))
